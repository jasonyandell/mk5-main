## Phase A1 — notrump diagnostic (CPU): VERDICT = ARTIFACT

margin:wp declared **notrump on 36% of contracts** (522/1438) in the Step-3 A/B. Is that real
(notrump genuinely wins under lens:ev) or an argmax artifact? Predicted (margin_net) vs empirical
(realized) exceedance, grouped by declared decl:

**On-policy (ab_snaps, margin:wp-declared, N=1081):**
| decl | n | made | pred≥30 / emp | pred≥35 / emp | pred≥40 / emp |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **notrump** | 513 | **0.40** | **0.70 / 0.44** | 0.51 / 0.30 | 0.32 / 0.19 |
| pip-trumps (agg) | 568 | ~0.60 | 0.71 / 0.63 | — | — |
- notrump predicted−empirical gap @30 = **+0.259**; pip-trumps = **+0.081**.

**Held-out corpus test split (unbiased, N=1088):**
| decl | n | made | pred≥30 / emp |
|---|---|---|---|
| **notrump** | 35 | 0.77 | **0.73 / 0.77** (gap −0.04) |
| pip-trumps (agg) | 1053 | ~0.45 | 0.54 / 0.53 (gap +0.01) |

**Verdict: ARTIFACT.** Notrump is *calibrated in-distribution* (the corpus's 35 notrump samples
are net:wp's selective, strong-hand declarations, realized 0.77) — but it is RARE (468/22k) and
appears only on strong hands, so the head learns a positive "notrump" offset. The argmax-over-decls
then **exploits** that offset: for many weaker hands the head rates notrump highest, margin:wp
declares it, and it realizes only 0.44 vs a predicted 0.70. This is the winner's curse at the
DECLARATION level, compounding the bidding-level over-bidding. No knob fixes it — on-policy
retraining (Phase B) will feed the head weak-hand notrump contracts realizing ~0.44 and kill the
offset. **Registered sub-prediction for Phase B: notrump share drops and its exceedance gap closes
round-over-round.** (General pip on-policy optimism is real but mild, +0.08; notrump +0.26 is the
dominant distortion.)
