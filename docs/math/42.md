# Texas 42: rules, worlds, and exact reduction

Jason Yandell · 18 October 2026 · CC0

**Scope.** Ordinary four-seat play, clockwise, partners opposite, seven tiles each, with a fixed declaration. All results below are derived; the rules and counting implementation are cited, not claimed mechanized. Special contracts require separate objectives and sometimes different active seats or winner rules.

## 1. Incidence, calling, and power

Let $P=\{0,\ldots,6\}$ and $D=\operatorname{Sym}^2(P)$, the unordered pairs with repetition. Then $|D|=28$. Natural suits $S_p=\{d:p\in d\}$ satisfy $|S_p|=7$ and $S_p\cap S_q=\{\{p,q\}\}$ for $p\ne q$: a covering, not a partition.

A declaration specifies a called set $K$ and powered set $T$. Pip trump $t$ gives $(S_t,S_t)$; doubles trump gives $(D^\circ,D^\circ)$; no trump gives $(\varnothing,\varnothing)$, where $D^\circ$ is the doubles. The additional doubles-suit case $(D^\circ,\varnothing)$ separates calling from power and supports Nel-O's different game.

Set $E_p=S_p\setminus K$ for $p<7$, and $E_7=K$. A lead $d$ selects

$$\ell(d)=\begin{cases}7&d\in K,\\\max(d)&d\notin K.\end{cases}$$

A leader may play any held tile. A follower with hand $H$ must play from $H\cap E_\ell$ if nonempty; otherwise any tile is legal.

Rank a called double $\{p,p\}$ by $p$ when $K=D^\circ$; otherwise rank doubles by $\top>12$ and mixed tiles by pip sum. Give powered tiles tier 2, remaining followers tier 1, and others tier 0. Compare positive tiers lexicographically by $(\text{tier},\text{rank})$; identify tier 0 at the bottom.

**Unique winner.** Every four distinct played tiles have exactly one greatest played tile under this order. The lead has positive tier. Each positive tier lies in one pip suit or the doubles suit; its mixed-tile sums are distinct, its sole pip double is highest, or its doubles have distinct pip ranks. Thus its maximum is unique. See the [rule specification](https://github.com/jasonyandell/mk5-main/blob/28edece47b5cf11d57f32095d835b82d7e169bf0/wiki/topics/suit-algebra-spec.md#5-following-and-the-led-suit).

## 2. A finite scored game

Write $ab$ for $\{a,b\}$. Set $c(55)=c(64)=10$, $c(50)=c(41)=c(32)=5$, and $c=0$ otherwise. The winner collects the trick's $1+\sum c(d)$ points and leads next. Seven tricks distribute $7+35=42$ points. If $P_0,P_1$ are partnership totals,

$$P_0-P_1=2P_0-42.$$

Each play removes one tile, so remaining hands, leader and trick prefix form a finite acyclic state graph. For a fully observed deal, backward induction with team-0 MAX and team-1 MIN solves additive point differential. Past score is unnecessary for that suffix objective; contract success needs a score or residual target. This omniscient recursion is not a decision rule for hidden hands. See the [play-phase construction](https://github.com/jasonyandell/mk5-main/blob/28edece47b5cf11d57f32095d835b82d7e169bf0/wiki/topics/play-phase-algebra.md).

## 3. Count worlds; preserve their mass

For distinguishable seats and unordered hands,

$$\#\text{hands}=\binom{28}{7}=1{,}184{,}040,\qquad
\#\text{deals}=\frac{28!}{(7!)^4}=472{,}518{,}347{,}558{,}400.$$

Given one's opening hand, $21!/(7!)^3=399{,}072{,}960$ completions remain. After a valid public history, let $U$ be unknown remaining tiles, $n_s$ hidden-seat capacities, and $A(d)$ the seats not forbidden to hold $d$ by observed failures to follow. The physical fiber $W$ has size

$$|W|=[\prod_s x_s^{n_s}]\prod_{d\in U}\left(\sum_{s\in A(d)}x_s\right).$$

**Proof.** Each product term assigns one permitted holder per tile; the coefficient enforces capacities. Failures to follow exclude retained suit tiles because hands only shrink. The fixed history must itself be valid, including plays of tiles subsequently revealed.

Equivalently, for ordered unknown tiles $d_1,\ldots,d_m$, count suffix allocations by

$$N(i,n)=\sum_{s\in A(d_i),\,n_s>0}N(i+1,n-e_s),\qquad N(m+1,n)=\mathbf1_{n=0}.$$

Contiguous blocks of these sizes give a bijection from integer ranks to worlds; a uniform rank samples uniformly. This is the [implemented counting recurrence](https://github.com/jasonyandell/mk5-main/blob/28edece47b5cf11d57f32095d835b82d7e169bf0/silas/src/physics.rs#L259-L340).

For nonempty $W$, a reduction $r:W\to C$ and statistic $q=\bar q\circ r$,

$$\mathbb E_{\mathrm{unif}(W)}q=\sum_{c\in C}\frac{|r^{-1}(c)|}{|W|}\bar q(c).$$

Partitioning the finite sum proves exactness. One representative per class suffices only for class-constant statistics, with class multiplicities retained; see [resolved beliefs](https://github.com/jasonyandell/mk5-main/blob/28edece47b5cf11d57f32095d835b82d7e169bf0/silas/src/belief.rs#L112-L155). Behavioral likelihoods generally change these weights.

Likewise, a structural symmetry need not preserve play. Pip permutations preserve incidence but may change the selected lead, rank and count. Clockwise seat rotations preserve play; odd rotations exchange teams and negate signed payoff. Reflections do not preserve the stipulated successor relation.
