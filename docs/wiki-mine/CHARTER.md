wiki's structure. This charter is the synthesis. The full pass should work from the

## The diagnosis (evidence in the five reports)

1. **The founding era is undigested.** Wiki epoch = b1de970 (2026-04-24); every
   wiki/sources digest postdates 2026-04-09. The 1,161 pre-wiki commits and 496
   pre-wiki beads issues (76% of the tracker) — solver birth, E[Q]'s deliberate
   anti-strategy-fusion design, the candlewax measurements, the capacity-ceiling
   null, the folk-wisdom refutations — exist only in git/beads/docs. (git-history.md,
   beads.md, forge.md, docs-readmes.md)
2. **Discoverability fails at era seams, not connectivity.** champion/jud ↔ burl =
   3 cross-links; distillation-lineage topics are a sealed island; 3 trails for 431
   pages, and the two healthy seams are exactly the two with trails. (wiki-audit.md)
3. **Vocabulary drift hides identity.** candlewax ≡ melted blob ≡ bimodal PDF had
   zero co-occurring pages before 2026-07-06. (wiki-audit.md §3)
4. **The wall itself** — "distill it, for what purpose? no idea what to do with
   distilled melted candlewax" — is the organizing question of the whole project and
   was nowhere stated. (Interim capture: topics/candlewax.md "The wall, stated
   precisely"; the full pass should give it page-zero weight.)
