**What I was doing.** Registering and grading unit paper 10 (16×8 s0 L1 1, #235).

**Where the skill failed me.** Following log 4, I checked the seen pooled rows by arithmetic, found
that on no-trump leads the unit moved nothing (±0.007), and read that as "on leads it acts only
through trump". The skill has no step for dose: the unit is mostly off on those leads (mean
activation 154 against 770 on discards). Ablating a near-silent unit moves nothing, whatever its
verb. The graded discard test missed for exactly that reason, and forcing it on at a follow dose
moved doubles on no-trump leads (−0.154).

**What I did instead.** Reported each cell's dose (on rate, mean activation) beside its effect.
Graded the miss as the finding. Read the null through a forced-on dose matched to where the unit acts.

**The change I would make.** In rung 1's causal bullet: report each row set's dose beside the
effect. An ablation null on low-dose rows says the unit is quiet there, not that its verb stops
there. Test the verb's reach by forcing the unit on at the dose where it works. Arithmetic from
seen rows settles the rows, not the verb.
