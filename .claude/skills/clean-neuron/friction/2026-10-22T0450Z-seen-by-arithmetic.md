**What I was doing.** Registering unit paper 9 (16×8 s0 L2 1, #235) after an exploratory read.

**Where the skill failed me.** "Register predictions before the run" says nothing about predictions
a pooled exploratory number already settles on a subset. Three of my four first predictions were
settled that way:
- a size and a rank on top-double leads followed by arithmetic from a pooled ablation: rows that
  cannot lead the tile dilute the mean, and a bound fixes the rank;
- one was in a previous paper's published `unit.json`.

The fresh Opus review caught all three. A "mostly seen" direction I kept as confirmatory then missed
on its base half: the net leads the 4-4 first, not the top double.

**What I did instead.** I moved the settled claims to "confirmatory, reported", rebuilt the graded
ones on unseen row sets (a joint ablation, a causal split by cell, a formation order), and graded the
confirmatory honestly as a miss.

**The change I would make.** Under "How to write the result", add: before sealing, for each
prediction, check whether any number already seen, on a superset of rows or in an earlier paper's
published JSON, settles it by arithmetic or a bound. If one does, it is confirmatory. "Mostly seen"
is unseen until its row set is measured.
