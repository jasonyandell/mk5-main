**Doing:** the #198 steering addendum, adding k·σ to named units among rows where they were off.

**Where the skill failed:** it says to test by steering, but not what to compare. I compared the
regret cost inside and outside a unit's concept. Those rows differ in difficulty before any
intervention (lead rows and last-seat rows carry different regret), so the comparison measured
the rows more than the unit. The result was near chance.

**What I did instead:** reported it as rung 1 (tools), unresolved, and filed matched-row steering
as #205.

**Change:** a causal comparison names its rows and matches them: the same seat in the trick, the
same number of legal moves, the same decisiveness, or a random unit at the same dose. Read
steering by where the move goes, not only by what it costs.
