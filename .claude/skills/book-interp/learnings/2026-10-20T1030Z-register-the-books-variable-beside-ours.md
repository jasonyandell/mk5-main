**Doing.** #201's confirmation: does the menu's doubles-trump cost live in hands with few top
doubles (thin/deep, a split I chose after looking)?

our pieces. The fresh-context review, reading Ch. 9 cold, caught it: the book's Hand 23 is thin and
its Hand 25 (no doubles hand, by the book) is deep. The review also found the encoding gap
(`ch9_ceiling` never prices trump losers, #230), which my "the reading is play" had skipped past.

**Did instead.** Registered the book's variables (the 6-6; the 6-6 and 5-5) blind beside ours.

read the encoding (does our player compute what the book counts first?) before writing "play".
