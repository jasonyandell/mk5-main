## Overview

One night, one piece of mathematics, seven pages. Jason's note *A minimal comparison algebra for
a declared 42 family* (2026-10-21) gave the formulation adopted in [[gus-historical-choice-wish]],
"what a sequence of choices means for the choices still ahead", an exact object: two histories
are one decision state when every future (legal menu, maximizing set) agrees, the coarsest right
congruence of the decision interface. Jason's steer for the night: lean into the math and the
engineering, train nothing, short capped tests, negative results are results, integrate with
[[silas]] if it keeps checking out, a ladder contestant only if feasible. The pages below are in
the order the night ran them. Every number carries its interval and baseline on its own page.

## The route

1. **[[gus-decision-quotient-2026-10-21]]** — the quotient measured on the saved 256-root
   contingent course of [[gus-tiny-gpt]]. 50,754 information nodes become 8,257 raw states, a
   median of about five nodes per state per root. Under what the student reads, no fresh root
   is ever a seen state. The tile-free fork interface finds 139 structures and 56 of 128 fresh
   roots seen, an upper bound because it cannot route. All 13 weak orders occur; ties dominate.
2. **[[comparison-algebra-2026-10-21]]** — the note made the project's own. In Lean
   (`scenario/lean/Stab/`): the hexagon, the residual congruence, the mixing boundary and the
   plan count, on the three standard axioms, with two of the night's own brief claims refuted
   (the 13 are sequential classes, not weak orders of the root triple; the ladder bound is n − 1
   blocks, not one). In Silas (`quotient.rs`, `family.rs`, `comparison.rs`): the quotient
   reproduces page 1 in every count, and the note's 560-deal ending is rebuilt on Silas's own
   rules with every figure matched, 24,089 nodes, 14 thresholds, 31 classes, 767 and 302 states,
   the message counts 1, 5, 11, 15, 23, 31. New: the path controllers have 142 and 132 states,
   good play meets only the knots of the optimum, and the comparison dimension is 168 at the
   root against 2 at the note's local history. Most of the 31-class atlas is counterfactual.
3. **[[gus-decision-class-probe-2026-10-21]]** — does the net already compute the class? No.
   Linear probes read neither the class nor the contrasts on fresh roots, for parent, students
   or untrained twins. The parent carries two-way flatness from pretraining (AUC 0.767, +0.157
   over the twins); the masked lessons weakened that readout and moved the three-way policy.
4. **[[gus-observation-labels-2026-10-21]]** — can a hand-relative, tile-free label route the
   fork? No. The finest of eight facts leaves 47 roots with a decision collision and sees 2 of
   128 fresh roots. What breaks it is the identity of an opponent's or the partner's tile.
5. **[[gus-posterior-labels-2026-10-22]]** — belief-derived labels (unseen trumps and count,
   voids, exhausted suits, rank among unseen, likelihood). The finest cuts decision collisions to
   7 and sees 0 of 128. Proved on the way: every label's quotient refines fork, so 56 of 128 is
   the ceiling for any label, and only the next decision's own answer, four symbols, reaches it.
6. **[[gus-joint-labels-2026-10-22]]** — the joint support of the unseen tiles. It is the first
   lawful label that routes every decision, at 8,425 states, more than naming the tiles, and it
   sees 0 of 128 fresh roots. The controls show the seats do no work: what routes is the per-play
   observation up to pip relabeling, not the posterior.
7. **[[walt-endgame-probe-2026-10-22]]** — is [[scenario-player|Walt]]'s endgame already the
   exact information-state optimum of its own model? Yes where it enumerates: 24,179 of 24,179
   decisions exact, with Silas agreeing with the Python reference at all 27,118. Where it
   samples with three tricks left it misses 5.6 to 5.7 percent of maximizing sets against about
   33 percent by chance, giving up at most 0.00098 success probability, under the registered
   0.001 bar. The misses are real, small errors, never ties broken badly.

## What the night established

- The decision state is small and exact, and the machinery to compute it is now native: Lean
  for the statements, Silas for the counts, with the Python reference kept as the oracle.
- For the Gus transfer wall, the finding is sharper than before. Fresh roots never repeat a raw
  state; the net does not carry the class; and no lawful coarse vocabulary in three label
  families compresses what routes a fresh decision, because what routes is the specific tiles
  observed. A student has to compute the next decision's answer from them, and nothing measured
  in it does so yet. That is a vocabulary and representation gap stated exactly, not a
  diagnosis of any one failed training gate.
- No ladder contestant. At four or fewer tiles Walt already holds the exact belief and plays the
  exact optimum of its model; the binding constraint is the model of the other seats, not the
  solver. Silas integration has one real gap, a Frame-to-Family builder with declared other-seat
  policies.
