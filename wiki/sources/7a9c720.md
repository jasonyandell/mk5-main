## Commit message

> feat(gus/eval): shine analysis — characterize where student is perfect
>
> Mirror of blunder forensics. Investigates the 73% of decisions where
> the student is already optimal (regret < 0.1 Q-pt). Writes a summary
> markdown with breakdowns by trick, declaration, legal count, and
> oracle E[Q] spread.
>
> Key findings on v2_voids_3000g_big:
>
>   Of 410 perfect decisions:
>     59.0% dead-ties (spread < 0.5) — any play works
>     15.6% moderate (0.5-5)
>     25.4% sharp-and-perfect (spread ≥ 5) — real skill
>
>   Zero-inference routing heuristic:
>     trust student when legal_count ≤ 2 OR decision_idx ≥ 22
>     → covers 450/560 decisions at ≤2% blunder rate
>     → only ~110 "wide-choice mid-game" decisions need the detector
>     → 4× reduction in detector workload
>
>   Distribution flip confirmed:
>     PERFECT owns late-game (dec 24-27 = 100%)
>     BLUNDER owns early-mid leads (dec 0, 4, 8 = 70% of blunders)
>
>   Sharp-and-perfect (the 104 "at its best"): spans all 10 declarations,
>   so wins are not coming from one easy declaration. Student is playing
>   real skill on high-spread positions.
>
> Implication: two-stage routing — cheap pre-filter on legal_count +
> decision_idx, then expensive detector only where needed. Zero cost on
> ~80% of decisions.

## What this commit establishes

≤ 2 OR decision_idx ≥ 22) that eliminates detector cost on 80% of decisions.
