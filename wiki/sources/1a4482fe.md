## What this commit establishes

[[convention-aware-blueprint-search]] is preserved as an IDEATED, unbuilt, and
unselected architecture. Its proposal uses a commonly known policy blueprint,
policy-likelihood belief filtering, and imperfect-information rollouts so a
public action can change a partner's later belief and response inside search.
It retains expert iteration, particle rejuvenation, calibration, rollout cost,
and convention drift as parts of the design rather than project findings.

The project-record refinement narrows the claim. [[forge|Forge]] Q already
prices ordinary within-world plans; the candidate mechanism is
information-set consistency and policy-aware partner response. The current
[[champion]] is not a suitable ready-made blueprint, the `39.184%` belief
ceiling does not measure action-conditioned inference, and a policy-matched
leaf is required. Single-agent search can read an existing convention but
cannot assume it will invent a new sender/receiver convention. SPARTA's fully
cooperative guarantee also does not transfer unchanged to two adversarial
partnerships.

The proposed clairvoyance ladder survives only as a consumer-specific
sensitivity atlas, not a universal hidden-information bound. A small gap does
not isolate convention value.

The open design question is whether concrete legal conventions from
[[w42-book-second-pass]] can seed a shared codebook while a learned policy
fills the rest of the action space, without treating the book as an optimal
full policy. No build or experiment is selected by this preservation pass.
