## Commit message

> feat(gus): v3 — V/π consistency regularizer
>
> Motivated by blunder forensics receipt #4: the V_head and π_me heads
> are decoupled enough that V can correctly predict the oracle's best
> legal E[Q] (e.g., +26) while π_me concentrates probability on a losing
> action (e.g., worth −0.4). The training objectives don't directly
> couple them — only indirectly via the shared encoder.
>
> v3_consistency_loss adds:
>
>     L_consistency = (V_head.detach() - Σ_legal softmax(π_me) · e_q)²
>
> V is detached so gradient only reshapes π. Forces policy-expected-Q
> (the Q the student would score if acting from its policy) to match
> V_head's prediction (the value V learned is "the best legal position
> value"). Concentrates π on actions that agree with V.
>
> Warmup from 0 → w_consistency over first 10 epochs so V_head can
> converge on its primary target before consistency pressure kicks in.
>
> Training script gus/train/train_v3_consistency.py — same args as
> train_v2_voids plus --w-consistency (default 0.3) and
> --consistency-warmup-epochs (default 10).
>
> Smoke-tested — loss integrates cleanly, finite gradient.
>
> Not yet run against a full corpus; will launch on 3000g when MPS is
> freed by the 10k gen. Follow-up experiment to see if eliminating the
> V/π decoupling closes the blunder tail.
