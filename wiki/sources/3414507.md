[[ls-mixture]] verbosity blender for the STaR corpus. Shortens `<|channel>thought` blocks while preserving all `tool_call`/`tool_response` envelopes and `commit_play`. Blends at 33% short ratio (above the LS-Mixture paper's 25–50% band, because Burl's failure mode is verbosity). A schema surprise discovered in [[sources/eebcae5]] later reveals that Gemma's chat template strips thought blocks before tokenization anyway, reframing the blend's benefit as coverage + regularization rather than verbosity reduction.
