## Training recipe

- **GPU:** [[modal]] B200 ($6.25/hr, ~3 min used)
- **Model:** `google/gemma-4-E2B-it`, bf16, SDPA attention, ClippableLinear patched
- **LoRA:** rank 16, alpha 32, targets all attn+MLP, dropout 0.05
- **Trainer:** SFTTrainer, 3 epochs, batch_size 2, grad_accum 4 (eff bs 8), LR 1e-4 cosine, warmup 0.1, gradient checkpointing
- **Data:** `burl/data/star_iter0_corpus.jsonl`, chat-format `{"messages": [...]}`
- **Special tokens preserved:** `<|channel>`, `<|tool_call>`, `<|tool_response>`

## Notes

- Loss values (20–50) look large because SFTTrainer reports sum-over-batch, not per-token mean. Trend and token_acc are the meaningful metrics.
- Epoch-boundary dips at steps 7, 14, 21 are partial-microbatch artifacts (50 % 8 = 2 leftover), not bugs.
- Default loss masking — trains on full sequence including user turn. Could flip `assistant_only_loss=True` for iter-1 per [[sft-completion-only-loss]].
