## Commit message

> eval(gus): LAMIR-primitive inference eval — direct π_me vs PIMC-via-Q
>
> Three inference modes compared on 560 held-out decisions:
>   direct (π_me argmax):                         65.4%
>   pimc-q (Q_head on 1 oracle-sampled world):    62.1%
>   pimc-belief (Q_head on 50 belief-sampled):    61.8%
>
> Direct π_me beats both PIMC variants because π_me IS the marginalized
> policy — it was trained directly on oracle's argmax(E[Q]) which already
> averages over thousands of consistent worlds. Adding 1-50 student-sampled
> worlds introduces variance without new information at the single-step level.
>
> LAMIR's real value comes from MULTI-step look-ahead with mid-tree belief
> updates — requires a π_opp head (not yet trained; needs opponent-view
> oracle queries in the corpus gen). Deferring that to a later session;
> this eval script is the harness it'll plug into.
>
> Per-decision breakdown shows PIMC occasionally beats direct on specific
> decisions (dec 3: 90/95/95; dec 14: 45/55/65; dec 15: 50/70/75) — hints
> of complementary signal that an ensemble or search could exploit.

Establishes the PIMC eval harness and confirms single-step look-ahead is net-negative
at this scale; multi-step requires the deferred π_opp head.
