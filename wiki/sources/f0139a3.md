## Commit message

> docs(gus): PRACTICALITIES.md — running receipts log
>
> Ten receipts from the overnight + morning session, capturing what we
> learned and re-learned:
>
> 1. Regret is vs oracle teacher (not theoretical optimum) — floor ~0.5-1
> 2. Q-MAE ≈ 10 is irreducible for imperfect-info distillation (σ≈20
>    intrinsic per-world variance); V-MAE and regret are the real metrics
> 3. Single-step PIMC underperforms direct π_me — strategy fusion
> 4. Data scaling dominates capacity at these sizes. 7.4M XL at 3000g
>    gave highest composite score but WORSE regret (1.54 vs 1.39) —
>    composite can disagree with the metric that matters
> 5. Regret distribution is bimodal: 73% perfect, 6% blunder tail
>    drives all of the 1.39 mean regret
> 6. Explicit void features barely help — transformer infers voids
>    attentionally from play tokens
> 7. MPS contention is ~30% wall overhead per process; parallel > serial
>    when wall-clock pressure is low
> 8. Gus has 32× raw training examples vs the Stage-1 oracle, but 100×
>    fewer distinct states — potential lever: gen 10 decls per seed
> 9. Joint-world tensor is the keystone; ~6 MB/game at SEM<0.5
> 10. Timeline calibration: 7-10 min gen, 10-90 min train on M5 Max
>
> Plus four open questions that surfaced without blocking.
