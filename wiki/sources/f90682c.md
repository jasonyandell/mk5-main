## Commit message

> feat(gus/eval): blunder detector + ensemble analysis
>
> Two parallel agents, one cohesive story.
>
> gus/eval/blunder_detector.py — tiny GradientBoostingClassifier that
> predicts from state-only features whether the student will blunder
> (regret > 8 Q-pt) at a given decision. Trained on 6k decisions from
> the 3000g corpus, tested on 3k disjoint decisions.
>
>   ROC-AUC: 0.926
>   At 15% flag rate: 80% recall of blunders
>   At 25% flag rate: 99% recall
>
> Dominant features: oracle_spread (max-min of legal E[Q]) and
> oracle_eq_std account for 77% of importance. Both derivable at
> inference from the student's own Q_head per-action outputs — a
> follow-up experiment will retrain with student features to measure
> deployment-realistic AUC.
>
> Business case: if we flag ~20% of decisions and replace them with
> oracle argmax (or any stronger fallback), mean regret drops from
> 1.23 → 0.46. Approaches the 0.5-1.0 Q-pt teacher-noise floor.
>
> gus/eval/eval_ensemble.py — compares 8 trained adapters under four
> ensembling strategies. Findings:
>
>   best single (v2_voids_3000g_big):   67.9% match, 1.39 regret
>   majority vote:                        69.1% match, 1.55 regret ← WORSE
>   softmax avg:                          71.3% match, 1.46 regret ← WORSE
>   v-weighted:                           71.3% match, 1.43 regret ← WORSE
>   oracle-per-decision (upper bound):  89.6% match, 0.36 regret ← CEILING
>
> Simple ensembles boost bot-match but hurt regret — averaging the
> confident-right adapter with less-confident ones. On near-ties the
> ensemble wins (match goes up); on sharp decisions the ensemble loses
> (regret goes up).
>
> The oracle-per-decision ceiling (0.36 regret, 74% reduction) shows
> real adapter diversity exists — 58% of decisions have disagreement,
> 24% have 3+ distinct actions. A router/gating model is the right
> shape, not an averaging ensemble.
>
> Detect-and-route is the emerging architecture: blunder detector as
> router, student as fast path, oracle/alt-adapter as fallback on
> flagged decisions. Projected regret ~0.5 Q-pt, near teacher-noise
> floor.

the right architecture. Detect-and-route emerges as the deployable shape.
