## Commit message

> feat(gus): full 4-head student — belief + V + π_me + world-conditioned Q
>
> Densely-supervised Q_head (3400× signal per decision vs belief-alone) plus
> V and π_me heads on the shared transformer encoder. World_encoder fuses a
> [28, 3] seat-one-hot layout into state_emb for the Q prediction.
>
> Empirical (100-game corpus, 20 epochs, MPS):
> - π_me bot-match (held-out): 57.9%   — up from 25% chance
> - belief top-1 (held-out):    34.5%  — near chance (data-limited)
> - V MAE:                      11.9   — on Q scale [-42, +42]
> - Q MAE (legal):              18.4
>
> Critically: train and eval π_me track within 1-2 points. The dense Q
> supervision regularizes the encoder — not overfitting like belief-only did.
>
> Per-decision:
>   dec  0 (first play):  π_me 20% (~chance, no info)
>   dec 13 (mid-game):    π_me 60%
>   dec 24-27 (end-game): π_me 100%
>
> Architecture is proven; next bottleneck is data. Chunked 1000-game
> generation (MPS can't handle n_games=1000 in one batch — INT_MAX dim
> limit) enables overnight scaling.
>
> Datasets now accept multiple .pt paths or globs, so chunked corpora
> merge transparently at load time.

Proves the 4-head architecture and establishes [[topics/dense-q-supervision]] as a
regularizer — train/eval π_me track within 1-2 points despite only 100 training games.
Datasets extended to accept glob paths for chunked corpus loading.
