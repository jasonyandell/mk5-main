## Key decision

vLLM is abandoned in favor of HF `model.generate()` with padded prompt batches. The root cause: Gemma 4 (released ~1 week before this commit) is too new for vLLM's pinned `transformers` version, causing tokenizer format conflicts that cannot be worked around without patching vLLM itself.

Trade-offs accepted:
- **Lost:** vLLM's continuous batching throughput and faster time-to-first-token
- **Lost:** the ~5-min vLLM engine cold-start is also eliminated (a wash on total iteration time)
- **Gained:** simplicity; no transformers version conflict; ClippableLinear patch conflict from [[68a0416]] disappears

The OVERVIEW notes vLLM as future work: "This will sort itself out as the ecosystem catches up."
