## Commit message

> feat(gus/eval): explanation sketcher for the visualizer
>
> Template-filled natural-language rationalization that reads the
> student's existing head outputs — no new ML. Plugs into
> play_visualizer.py as a blockquote line below each "Student
> brainstate" section.
>
> Template slots: confidence (pi_peak, pi_entropy), top-2 actions,
> belief top-2 (domino, seat), V/π disagreement flag, Q-spread risk
> language, and labels for forced / end-game / blunder-hotspot
> patterns.
>
> Example outputs (game 0):
>
> Confident-correct:
>   picks 2-2 (p=0.71; second-best 5-5 p=0.29). Expects -5.6 Q
>   (oracle top: -5.9); high variance — Q could swing ±10.9 across worlds.
>
> V/π disagreement (the pathology the consistency loss targets):
>   splits: 6-5 (p=0.38) vs 5-5 (p=0.22). Expects -1.0 Q (oracle top:
>   -7.7) — V/π disagreement: V says -1.0 but π's pick only scores
>   -10.5; moderate risk. Believes partner holds 6-4 (p=0.58),
>   left-opp holds 6-1 (p=0.51).
>
> Forced move:
>   _forced move_ — confidently picks 4-2 (peak 1.00). Expects -7.7 Q
>   (oracle top: -9.7).
>
> Template catches the signal-rich cases (V/π disagreement, splits,
> high variance) crisply. Mechanical on dead-ties where there's
> genuinely nothing to say — arguably correct behavior.
>
> Updated play_visualizer.py to sample K=8 worlds for Q_head std at
> the chosen action (for the "risk" language). Adds one new blockquote
> per decision; no layout changes.
>
> A full-ML rationale head would add causal language, plan-horizon
> reasoning, comparative rationales, adaptive verbosity. This template
> is a diagnostic layer and cheap daily-driver; ML head is the
> storyteller upgrade.
