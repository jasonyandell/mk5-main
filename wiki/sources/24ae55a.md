## Key doc additions

### Stage 0 v1 what-we-built

Narration generator, rules primer (55/55 facts verified), Q&A corpus (3500 examples, 7 categories), Stage 0 LoRA adapter trained on [[modal]] L4, pushed to HuggingFace as [[stage-0-adapter]]. Wandb: `jasonyandell-forge42/lem-stage0`.

### Training recipe (canonical reference)

Five-step recipe documented as ground truth for reproducibility:

1. Monkey-patch `Gemma4ClippableLinear` → `nn.Linear` before loading (see huggingface/peft#3129)
2. Use bf16 (not fp16 — GradScaler fails with gradient checkpointing)
3. `gradient_checkpointing=True`, `use_reentrant=False` (model ~20GB bf16, L4 22GB VRAM)
4. `eval_strategy="no"` — eval OOMs on L4; training converges to 100% accuracy by step 50
5. 1 epoch sufficient; loss 32 → 15 → 2.8 → 0.001 in 40 steps

### Key insight

Trump membership did not transfer from Q&A format to narration-context reasoning. Per the OVERVIEW: "Trump rules are the kind of thing you learn by playing more games and getting corrected — which is what Stage 1 (STaR) does." See [[learned-by-playing]].
