## What this commit establishes

[[gus]] is declared as a third sibling project alongside [[lem]] and [[burl]]. Rather than
reasoning via language or tool calls, Gus trains a small neural network — shared transformer
trunk + three heads (policy / value / belief) — directly against [[forge]]'s E[Q] framework oracle.

Key premises:
- LEM stalled at 55% bot-match (reasoning-verification ceiling)
- Burl's iter-3-rules reached 90% bot-match but STaR iteration converged on noise
- The oracle gives variance-free E[Q] labels; this is a "massive, underrated advantage"
- Training belief supervised against truth (not self-play distillation) should exceed [[zeb]]'s 39%

## Architecture declared

```
Shared transformer trunk (public_history + your_hand + belief_features)
├── Policy head  — π(a | state, belief), softmax over legal dominoes
├── Value head   — V(state, belief), scalar trained to oracle E[Q]
└── Belief head  — P(unseen domino ∈ {L, partner, R}), supervised at train time
```

Optional PIMC leaf search at inference when policy entropy is high (Bridge-AI recipe).
LEM as commentary head deferred until the player works.
