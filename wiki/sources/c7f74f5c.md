## What this commit establishes

PR 39 ends at research readiness. The repaired sampler, canonical decision
records, joined failure atlas, provenance contract, result vocabulary, and
cumulative evidence map make future experiments attributable; they do not
select the next mechanism, causal microgame, or successor architecture.

The prior Gus synthesis was overbroad. Every tested multi-step LAMIR-1 rollout
mode lost to direct `pi_me`, but [[gus-qmean-router]] is bounded positive
evidence for selective consumption: belief-sampled Q-mean modestly improved
regret, and a learned second-opinion router improved it further on a small
held-out set. The result has no marks-to-7 validation and does not choose a
direction, but it remains part of the evidence ledger.

Natural partnership legibility is broader than deliberate signaling. Ordinary
good play can incidentally reveal holdings, priorities, and intention to a
partner familiar with the policy. Explicit reliable signals are sparse while
weak contextual hints are common; their aggregate marks value is unmeasured.
The existence of signaling therefore does not establish it as the current
attack on [[the-wall]].

Finally, a strategic non-partnership mechanism may break the wall without
producing a matched-vs-shuffled interaction. Fixed-pair lift remains mandatory
for a [[partnership-value]] claim, not for every registered research direction.
