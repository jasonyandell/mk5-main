## Commit message

> feat(gus): arena game-level eval + play visualizer
>
> Two new evaluation tools from parallel-agent work:
>
> - gus/eval/arena.py — full-game simulation with the student at one seat
>   and E[Q] bot at the others. Reuses forge/eq/ GPU pipeline as the engine,
>   replaces select_actions for the student's seat with a neural inference
>   call. Reports contracts made/set, avg bidder/defender points, and a
>   direct comparison to the all-E[Q]-bot baseline on the same seeds.
>
>   Pilot run (20 games, 2 seeds × 10 decls, seeds 900020-900021) on
>   v2_voids_3000g_big:
>     student at seat 0 → made 10/20 contracts (50%)
>     all-bot baseline  → made 16/20 contracts (80%)
>     avg bidder points: 21.2 (student) vs 29.6 (bot), −8.4/hand
>   Game-level gap is bigger than decision-level regret suggested —
>   1.39 Q-pt regret compounds to ~30 pp contract-made drop.
>
> - gus/eval/play_visualizer.py — renders a single game as readable markdown.
>   Per-trick table (actual play / student pick / oracle pick / regret badge
>   ✓▽⚠🔥), student brainstate per decision (legal E[Q], π probs, V_head,
>   top-3 belief), final score + blunder histogram.
>
> Blunder forensics deliverable lives in scratch/BLUNDER_FORENSICS.md
> (gitignored) — surfaced the V/π-head decoupling that motivates the
> next training experiment.
