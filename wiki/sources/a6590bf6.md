## What this commit establishes

[[w42-book-second-pass|Winning 42]] can be treated as a source of sparse
coordinated policy overlays. Installing the same explicit convention on sender
and receiver supplies a shared codebook before unilateral search; a learned
policy remains responsible for the rest of the state space. This is a
bootstrap mechanism, not a claim that the book is an optimal or complete
blueprint.

Double-dummy endorsement is not a convention classifier. Good technique can
also convey information that changes a partner's later play. The preserved
attribution is therefore a sender-overlay x partner-reader x opponent-reader
factorial. It separates direct action value or sacrifice, the partner decoding
interaction, the cost of opponent decoding, and net paired marks. An
oracle-disapproved sacrifice rescued by a decoder is a particularly clean
positive example, but not the definition of convention value.

The book also supplies opponent action-likelihood hypotheses. Because actual
opponents may not follow it, book-likeness remains a latent policy type inferred
from public actions rather than a population-wide assumption. The same
legibility that helps a partner may help both opponents more.

The initial extraction surface includes top-unplayed-trump/donation coupling,
donate-highest, dump-to-inform and 84 keep priorities, auction messages, and
the separate Plunge/Splash signaling variant. Density, coverage, calibration,
and net marks value remain unmeasured. The candidate is now visible in
[[partnership-wall-research]] and constrained by
[[partnership-research-gates]], but no experiment or architecture is selected.
