## Why it failed

Per [[sources/26f5ddf]]'s commit message: `Gemma4ForConditionalGeneration` does not support LoRA in vLLM 0.19.0. The multimodal weight layout is incompatible with merged checkpoints. Inference-only vLLM would work, but the [[star-harness]] loop requires loading an adapter after each LoRA training step — which vLLM cannot do for this model class.
