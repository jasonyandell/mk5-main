## What this commit establishes

[[belief-weighted-jud-mcts]] is a surviving hypothesis built from independently
supported pieces. [[w42-jud-v1]]'s JudSearch improved greedy Jud play by `+2.28`
marks/game, proving that search can turn the realized-value post-trick leaf into
a much better move ranking. Its worlds sweep added only `+0.11`; flat sample
count was not the lever. MCTS adds adaptive branch allocation and continuation
depth rather than merely more worlds.

Node identity separates two designs. A determinized MCTS uses weighted root
particles but permits separate hidden-world continuations, retaining strategy
fusion. Information-set MCTS shares action statistics across worlds the acting
policy cannot distinguish and reweights later-seat beliefs after simulated
public actions. That structural version can price action-derived inference,
plan persistence, and partner response.

The compact candidate stack is:

`belief particles -> information-set MCTS -> blueprint policy -> Jud V_realized leaf`

The blueprint supplies action likelihoods and response semantics; MCTS supplies
search allocation; Jud supplies the policy-conditioned realized leaf. Zeb is
supporting MCTS prior art, LAMIR supplies the scalar-leaf and rollout-compounding
warning, and neither tested this combination.

J0-J4 separately attributes current JudSearch, root belief weighting, adaptive
determinized depth, information-set node sharing with mid-tree belief updates,
and the convention blueprint. The idea is visible in
[[partnership-wall-research]] and has distinct promotion gates; no bundled
result can silently turn depth, belief, and convention into one explanation.
