## Trump drilling — 5 question types (see [[trump-drilling]])

1. `is_trump` — "Is 6-4 trump when fives are trump?" (No — pips are 6 and 4, neither is 5). Targets the persistent 6-4 error.
2. `list_trumps` — "List all trumps when fours are trump." Full enumeration.
3. `which_trumps` — batch classification of multiple dominoes in one prompt.
4. `trump_or_follow` — "Did they follow suit or play a trump?" Distinguishes following from trumping.
5. `count_trump` — "Is the 5-5 also a trump when doubles are declared?" Tests doubles-trump interaction.

Total new examples: 5k. Combined with [[kerry-curriculum]] 15k = 20k total corpus for [[v3-adapter]].

Training: 200 steps on [[modal]] B200. Results in [[8c1bb14]].
