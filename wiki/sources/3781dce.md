## Design principle added to OVERVIEW

> "Go with the model's grain; catch it doing right. Small models have their own instincts — Gemma reaches for a `play` verb even when our menu doesn't define one... If `play` is what Gemma wants, `play` is what we give it. STaR trains the model's own best behavior back into itself — their words, their corrections, their self-checks."
