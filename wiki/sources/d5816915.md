## What this commit establishes

[[convention-aware-blueprint-search]] is a surviving hypothesis with a positive
structural case. It creates an in-search channel from public action to partner
belief and response; [[w42-book-second-pass|Winning 42]] supplies coordinated
sender/receiver initialization; existing sampler, information-set, record, and
arena infrastructure supplies much of the measurement path; and the
sender-by-reader factorial can attribute technique, partnership response,
four-seat inference, and net marks.

No experimental evidence changed. The prior wording repeated implementation
status and caveats often enough to imply a negative verdict that the project
does not possess. The revised page records IDEATED and unbuilt once, then lets
the engineering requirements and [[partnership-research-gates]] carry the
scientific boundaries.

Opponent readability is part of the opportunity as well as the accounting.
Published conventions provide an initial policy hypothesis for every seat;
latent policy types allow adherence to be inferred from public history. The
same action can coordinate a partner, reveal an opponent holding, and invite
counter-inference, all within one measurable design.

The hypothesis remains alongside target granularity, auction decoding,
information-set planning, and distributional utility. It now has durable
research-trail visibility proportional to its supporting logic rather than to
an experiment that has not yet run.
