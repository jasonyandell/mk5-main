> **Compute setup that works:**

> - Inference: Modal H100 ($3.95/hr) — sequential HF generate, ~1 prompt/min
> - Full STaR iteration: Modal H100, all phases, ~15 min, ~$1

## What didn't work

- **vLLM:** Gemma 4 too new (~1 week old at time of writing); version conflicts between vLLM's pinned `transformers` and Gemma 4's tokenizer format. Fell back to sequential HF `generate()`. Filed as future work — expected to resolve as ecosystem catches up. See [[sources/8724e93]].
- **Local CUDA:** llama-cpp-python CUDA build failed (GCC 13 vs CUDA 12.3 incompatibility on WSL2). CPU inference works at 11 tok/s. All GPU work routed to [[modal]].

## Key insight named

The commit names the [[decisions/discard-illegal-traces]] policy as a "ClaudeAI insight" and documents its diagnostic interpretation: 40% illegal in iteration 0 means Stage 0 rules knowledge is still shaky; the rate should drop as the model internalizes rules through practice.
