## Commit message

> fix(lamir1): Bug 5 — use per-world game_hands for opp simulated plays
>
> When opp tokens were built for LAMIR-1 rollout, slot→domino lookup used
> the real deal hands. But the slot was chosen by π_me against a world hand
> that may have a different domino at that slot, creating an incoherent
> (tokens, world_assign) pair. Fix: precompute per-world 4-player game_hands
> (_world_game_hands) and pass them to all _build_tokens_voids calls inside
> lamir1_decision — both opp step queries and the leaf V_head query.
