## Commit message

> feat(gus/model): lazy IterableDataset for train corpora at arbitrary scale
>
> Adds JointWorldFullIterable alongside the eager JointWorldFullDataset.
> Streams chunks one at a time through a shuffle buffer; memory footprint
> is bounded by (one chunk + buffer), regardless of corpus size.
>
> Motivation: train_v3_consistency OOM'd eagerly loading the full 10k
> corpus (~110 GB). Observed RSS ceiling on the new class: ~3.4 GB for
> 5 chunks, and the ceiling does NOT grow with chunk count.
>
> Training scripts gain --lazy (opt-in) plus --buffer-size and
> --length-cache. Default behavior unchanged — existing scripts and
> eval tools on small corpora keep using the eager class.
>
> Smoke-tested: 2 epochs over 5 chunks (14k items) in 6s wall,
> memory flat across epochs, item count matches __len__.

## What this commit establishes

Removes the memory ceiling on corpus scale: RSS bounded at ~3.4 GB regardless of how
many chunks are streamed. Unblocks v3 consistency training on the 10k corpus (~110 GB)
that OOM'd with the eager loader.
