## Commit message

> feat(gus): LAMIR-1 rollout — argmax opp π_me, V_head leaf
>
> 1-ply look-ahead: for each legal action, simulate remaining trick plays
> using rotation-equivariant π_me as π_opp (no π_opp head needed), then
> score with V_head at leaf averaged over M corpus worlds.
>
> Result on 560 held-out decisions vs baseline (direct π_me):
>   regret:    2.384 (baseline 0.551) — worse
>   bot-match: 62.86% (baseline 76.07%) — worse
>
> The rollout actively hurts. Damage is concentrated at trick_pos 0-2
> (leader and early followers). Trick_pos 3 fallbacks (direct π_me)
> are unchanged. The learning: argmax opp simulation via π_me creates
> adversarial leaf states that don't reflect oracle-equilibrium play,
> and V_head correctly evaluates those worse leaf states — causing
> the action scoring to diverge from e_q ordering.
