## Commit message

> docs(gus): PRACTICALITIES receipts 11-13 + OVERVIEW status update
>
> Receipts 11-13 capture today's parallel-agent findings:
>
>   11. Arena: 1.39 Q-pt decision regret → 18 pp game-level win-rate gap.
>       Student team wins 52% vs all-oracle 70%. Real but weaker player.
>
>   12. Naïve ensembling hurts regret. Simple voting/softmax-average over
>       8 adapters boosts bot-match but increases regret — averages away
>       the confident-right adapter on sharp decisions. Oracle-per-decision
>       ceiling is 0.36 regret (74% reduction) — massive latent diversity.
>       Router, not ensemble.
>
>   13. Detect-and-route. Small GBM classifier flags blunders from student-
>       only features at ROC-AUC 0.839. At 20% flag rate: regret 1.13 → 0.49.
>       Top feature: pi_peak (0.19) — when π is uncertain, trigger fallback.
>       Student's Q_head spread is a weaker proxy than hoped (Q trained on
>       one world per forward; fix: multi-world variance reg or K=50 at
>       inference).
>
> Plus an "emerging architecture" section documenting detect → route →
> fallback as the deployable shape of Gus v1.0.
>
> OVERVIEW.md status banner updated with current numbers, next levers,
