## Commit message

> docs(gus): belief is at Bayes ceiling — PRACTICALITIES §21, diagnostic
>
> Computed Bayes-optimal belief top-1 from oracle's own sampled worlds on
> corpus_eval_20.pt: 39.184%. Gus v3 belief head matches this within noise
> (~38-39% per §6). The "belief is hard" intuition was correct — but the
> cause is that the signal isn't there (especially at d_idx 0-5 where
> early-hand belief is ~33% = pure prior), not architecture or capacity.
>
> Reorients belief roadmap: top-1 accuracy is solved. Remaining lever is
> posterior shape (calibration). §15 already showed distribution-target
> training closes 47% of the KL gap; the open loop is co-training belief
> + world_encoder + Q_head jointly so that better calibration propagates
> to look-ahead value estimates.
