## What this commit establishes

Four-agent adversarial review of PR 39 found that the shipped
`uniform-completion-dp-v1` repair was itself silently non-uniform on MPS: the
backend's int64 `gather` kernel rounds 62-bit values through float32
(reproduced: errors ~`1e11`), so every emitted world stayed valid while the
distribution collapsed — support `24/60`, chi-square `1.4e5` (df 59) at 60,000
samples on the audited bias fixture, worse than the legacy sampler it
replaced, on the device [[burl]] runs on. All PR validation had run
`--device cpu`. The `where` selection is bit-identical on CPU, so the
checked-in fixed-seed audit artifacts remain valid; the new `mps` test case
fails against the old selection and passes against the fix.

The same review completed the trail's mechanism-level prior sweep
([[w42-champion-selfplay-fixed-point]], [[lamir1-ceiling]],
[[strategy-fusion]], [[past-belief-future-direction]], [[pi-opp-head]], the
Plunge/Splash testbed), registered the clairvoyance decomposition as the cheap
partnership-value existence bound, and rebalanced the entry pages so the
coordination family reads as one registered direction on [[the-wall]] rather
than its center of gravity.
