## Commit message

> feat(gus): v2 — engine-computed void features fed to state encoder
>
> VoidsEncoder projects a [24]-dim (3 opponents × 8 suits) void indicator
> vector into d_model and adds it to the pooled state_emb before the
> four heads. Void inference is a simple rule-check: per-trick, compute
> led suit from the leader's domino; any non-leader that didn't play a
> containing-the-led-suit (non-trump) domino is marked void in that suit.
>
> Empirical (1000g corpus, d=192, 4 layers, 40 epochs):
> - π_me:  66.1% (v1) → 65.2% (v2)    ~flat
> - belief: 37.2% → 38.6%             +1.4pp
> - vMAE:   7.6  → 7.7                flat
> - qMAE:  12.3  → 12.2               flat
>
> Modest — the transformer was already inferring voids attentionally from
> the play tokens. Explicit features close a small gap but aren't a big
> unlock. Keep v2 in the repo as a cleaner baseline; real next-lever is
> more data + bigger model.
