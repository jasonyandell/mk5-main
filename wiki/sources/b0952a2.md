> feat(burl): spike_drivers — empirical mode-catalyst dominoes on eq_outcome_distribution
>
> For bimodal distributions, reports which (seat, domino) assignments are
> over-represented in each spike. Answers "when this play lands in the
> disaster branch, who tends to be holding what?"
>
> Example seed 900013: shape=bimodal, spike@Q=-5: partner.d14 lift=2.00,
> spike@Q=+8: partner.d5 lift=2.00. "If partner has 5 you win; d14 you lose."

Adds `spike_drivers` to `OutcomeDistribution`: for each mode, compute per-(seat,domino) lift over background frequency. Motivated by grain-recon: Gemma articulates decisions in bid-satisfaction vocabulary ("probability of meeting the bid") not raw PDF shape — spike drivers surface the same information in Gemma's native vocabulary. Noise caveat: reliable at N≥50 worlds per spike.
