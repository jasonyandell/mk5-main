## What this commit establishes

jud's one-organ move is machinery, not vocabulary: one net serves bidding
(the v0 ValueBidder mechanism of [[w42-jud-v0]] unchanged, via a
signature-compatible `pmake_table`) and play (`judplay`), with bid-time as the
empty-history special case of one featurization — byte-identity with
`margin_net`'s root encoding and between train (snapshot prefix) and serve
(live engine state) both pinned by test. The [[arena]] now emits per-decision
corpora compactly (one play-history field per hand). Round-0 grades honestly:
the searchless greedy player loses big to the oracle-backed `lens:ev` while
beating random play, and the bidder replays v0's round-0 winner's curse —
the v1 loop is the registered climb.
