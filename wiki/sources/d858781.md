Introduces [[belief-trajectory]] tool (761 LOC) in `burl/tools/belief_trajectory.py`, bridging [[gus]]'s calibrated belief head into Burl's tool surface. The `_gus_adapter.py` module converts Burl's duck-typed `game_state` to Gus's seat-symmetric tokenizer format via a `_ShimDecision` record walk. Dual output (dict + LLM-legible prose with STRONG/MEDIUM/WEAK tiers) surfaces Gus's V value, CLS attention tokens, and per-domino posteriors. Wired into [[wax-museum]] as a free (non-advancing) side-call at every gate state.
