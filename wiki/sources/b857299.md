> feat(lem): Stage 0 v7→v9 — structured reasoning templates + verifier
>
> Curriculum arc expanding comprehension from 8 to 14 categories, exploring
> how structured-template training propagates into open-ended rationalization.
>
> New generators (generate_comprehension.py):
> - conditional_beat (v7): "Can X beat Y when Z is led?" with structured
>   engine-derivable answer template. Forces multi-step reasoning with
>   verifiable sub-steps.
> - beaters_in_unseen, partner_response (v8): reason over the unseen pool
> - intervention_check (v8): play-order matters — partner can only beat an
>   opponent who plays BEFORE partner in the trick sequence
> - visibility_audit (v9): enumerate visible + unseen dominoes
> - highest_unseen_in_suit (v9): single-fact supporting category
>
> New modules:
> - generate_decisions.py: decision extractor with E[Q] data + structured
>   state for the verifier
> - scout_play_qwen.py: runs rationalization experiments on Qwen
> - verify_rationalization.py: 6 engine-verified checks (domino validity,
>   references-visible, hand claims, trump declaration, trump membership,
>   action match). Filter that makes hallucinations irrelevant for training.
>
> Graders (grade_offline.py): added for all 6 new categories.
>
> Findings (see lem/OVERVIEW.md for full details):
> - v7: conditional_beat template propagates into rationalization context
>   it was never trained on — real transfer.
> - v8: adding 3 categories together shifted response distribution to
>   verbose-by-default; concepts synthesize across categories.
> - v9: full 3-epoch train hits 83% overall on 14 categories. Rationalization
>   pass rate plateaus at ~68/100 across all versions (1.7B capacity ceiling?).
> - visibility_audit at 0%: long-enumeration answers fail autoregressive
>   truncation. Single-fact supporting categories (highest_unseen_in_suit,
>   100%) are the reliable pattern.
> - Compound tasks (beaters_in_unseen, partner_response) didn't improve
>   from adding atomic supporting categories — composition doesn't auto-emerge.
>
> Final adapter: jasonyandell/qwen3-1.7b-texas42-stage0-v9
