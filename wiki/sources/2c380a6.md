## Commit message

> fix(lamir1): Bug 6 — zero played dominoes from world_assign at Q_head leaf
>
> After 1-3 opp rollout plays, the world_assignment tensor passed to the leaf
> Q_head still reflected the pre-rollout hand layout. This gave Q_head stale
> beliefs: dominoes that were played out of world hands were still marked as
> present in opp hands.
>
> Fix: during the rollout loop, record the domino ID played by each opp in each
> world. After the loop, zero those rows in world_assign_leaf before the Q_head
> call. Matches the training convention in dataset_seq_world._world_to_assignment
> where played dominoes are absent from the assignment tensor.
>
> Applied to both lamir1_qleaf_decision and lamir1_piopp_decision.

## What this commit establishes
