## Commit message

> docs(gus): PRACTICALITIES receipt 15 — belief calibration doesn't propagate
>
> Frozen-trunk fine-tune of belief head with distribution target
> (empirical marginal over M sampled worlds, not one-hot truth).
>
> Isolated:
>   truth top-1: 38.4% → 38.3% (unchanged)
>   KL vs world-marginal: 0.078 → 0.062 (47% closer to perfect)
>
> Downstream:
>   pimc-belief K=50: 66.4% → 65.4% (regressed 1pp)
>   blunder detector AUC: flat
>   blunder detector PR-AUC: regressed
>
> The calibration is real but doesn't propagate because Q_head was
> co-trained with mode-sharp belief samples; softening at inference
> creates distribution shift downstream heads weren't prepared for.
>
> Takeaway: need to CO-TRAIN {belief, world_encoder, Q_head} together
> with distribution-belief + regular Q loss. Stacking calibration on
> a frozen ecosystem doesn't work.
>
> Not promoted. Ablation result. Kept in scratch/.

## What this commit establishes

for distributional belief to benefit PIMC.
