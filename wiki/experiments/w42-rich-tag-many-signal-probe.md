## Summary

[[w42]] has a paired rich-tag many-signal probe over the same small corpus split
used by [[w42-raw-public-state-baseline]] and
[[w42-v0-strategy-tags-baseline]].

- script: `w42/rich_tag_many_signal_probe.py`
- artifacts: `w42/rich_tag_many_signal_probe/`
- W&B run: `https://wandb.ai/jasonyandell-forge42/w42/runs/3xyy2dxr`
- feature delta: adds public-state-safe rich global/action signals derived from
  the v0 detector tensors and the [[w42-strategy-tags-v1-map]] concept buckets
- data slice: 100 generated train games and 20 held-out eval games from the
  local [[gus]] joint-world corpora

On 560 held-out eval decisions, the rich model's best and final epoch reached
1.970 mean regret, 65.54% oracle-best action match, 75.54% near-tie rate, and
11.43% tail regret rate at regret >= 5. This beats the prior v0 best by a small
margin on mean regret (1.970 vs 2.000) and match (+1.61 pp), and beats the
reloaded raw final checkpoint by a larger margin (2.834 -> 1.970 mean regret).
The run remains far behind `E[Q] N=10` at 0.118 mean regret.

## Key Question

Do many cheap, public-state-safe chapter-bucket signals help the small w42 policy
model beyond raw public state and the v0 strategy-tag baseline, or do they add
noise on the established small split?

## Method

| field | value |

| report owner bead | `t42-csw6.12` |

| statistical test | held-out mean regret, match, near-tie, tail-regret rate, concept-bucket slices |
| success criterion | paired report-only evidence for whether rich cheap signals help or add noise |

The rich model keeps the raw/v0 baseline's core transformer, void encoder,
candidate-action embedding, action hidden size, optimizer, epoch count, batch
size, and random seeds. The architecture delta is limited to two extra public
tag adapters: 48 rich global signals and 48 rich action-local signals.

## Feature / Detector Set

| feature or detector | scope | public-state safe? | concept bucket | source |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `tokens`, `attention_mask`, `voids`, `candidate_action_id` | raw baseline | yes | raw public state | [[w42-raw-public-state-baseline]] |
| `strategy_features`, `strategy_action_features` | v0 baseline | yes | cheap v0 tag groups | [[w42-strategy-tags-v0]] |
| `rich_global_signals` | global derived features, 48 dims | yes | bidding risk, count, trump, pounce, scoring proxies | `rich_tag_many_signal_probe.py` |
| `rich_action_signals` | action-local derived features, 48 dims | yes | donation, off protection, pounce, no-trump/doubles, walker/endgame proxies | `rich_tag_many_signal_probe.py` |

The rich signals are aggregates and interactions over the public v0 tensors:
legal action summaries, count-pressure windows, donation windows, double
protection, suit/trump pressure, late-hand gates, and v1-style report buckets.
They do not implement hidden-hand detector truth, proof search, Burl trace
review, or oracle counterfactual detectors.

## Caveats

- This is a single-seed research run on only 560 eval decisions.
- The rich feature surface adds parameters as well as signals, so the result is
  not a pure causal ablation of tags.
- Concept buckets are diagnostic public proxies, not claim-specific detectors.
- Tail regret is feasible for the reloaded raw checkpoint and rich model, but
  prior raw-best and v0 artifacts did not store tail metrics.
- The first live W&B run `c5x85xfx` was superseded by `3xyy2dxr` after a
  tail-delta label was corrected; committed artifacts correspond to `3xyy2dxr`.
