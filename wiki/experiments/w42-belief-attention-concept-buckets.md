## Public-State Boundary

Online-safe inputs:

- ruleset/scoring metadata, public auction, bids, passes, declaration, trick
  history, captured count, current trick winner, legal action set, current
  player's own hand, candidate-action facts, and public void events;
- public priors derived from bid amount, seat/order, failed bids, score context,
  and legal play history.

Report-only inputs:

- hidden owner labels, partner/opponent private hands, true partner support,
  completed-hand attribution, forge oracle values, paired counterfactuals, Burl
  trace text, synthetic contaminated traces, and leakage-audit labels.

Public-state-only limit: a live feature may say that public evidence makes a
void, suit strength, partner weakness, or pounce risk plausible. It may not use
the true hidden hand to state that the inference is factually correct. Hidden
facts are valid for evaluation, calibration, leakage probes, and after-the-fact
reports only.

## Caveats

- This is a report-only synthesis from existing artifacts, not a new model run.
- Existing numeric slices are single-seed, 560-decision, v0 proxy slices.
- No belief calibration outputs were available for void inference, bid-derived
  priors, or early-trick discovery.
- No Burl traces were reviewed, so trace faithfulness and table-talk leakage
  remain pending.
- Public-state tags can encode legal evidence and priors only; true hidden-hand
  ownership is evaluation/report-only.
- Claim-ledger statuses remain unchanged.
