## Summary

This report validates the [[winning42-ch09-doubles-no-trump]] regime claims that
are testable without a generated game corpus: doubles-as-trump suit membership,
standard no-trump suit membership, the non-standard separate-doubles-suit variant
gate, and exact static hand-shape buckets for doubles/no-trump regime switching.

Epistemic status: confirmed result for deterministic ruleset/regime predicates;
exact enumeration for static hand proxies; underpowered for strategy choices that
need [[forge]] E[Q], [[gus]]/w42 model rows, or [[burl]] trace review.

Headline: all five deterministic Chapter 9 regime gates passed. Exact enumeration
over all 1,184,040 seven-tile hands supports the book's warning that doubles count
is only a candidate signal: only 4.316% of hands hold 4+ doubles, but among those
candidate hands 41.541% miss `6-6`, 69.660% miss at least one top double among
`6-6`/`5-5`, and 17.532% still carry at least 10 off-count points. The same-hand
no-trump-over-doubles-trump proxy bucket is measurable but remains `underpowered`
until oracle rollouts compare declarations on the same hand.

## Key Question

Which Chapter 9 doubles-as-trump and no-trump claims can be validated now with
ruleset predicates and exact hand enumeration, and which claims must wait for
oracle/model/trace evidence?

## Findings

| finding | evidence | metric | status |
|---|---|---|---|
| Doubles-as-trump and standard no-trump regime predicates are internally supported. | `ruleset_regime_checks.csv` | 5 / 5 checks passed | supported |
| Doubles leave native suits under doubles-trump; no-trump keeps doubles native. | `ruleset_regime_checks.csv` | 28 / 28 membership cases; 7 / 7 follow-removal cases; 7 / 7 no-trump native-top cases | supported |
| `6-5` is the exact dual-suit top non-double under doubles-as-trump. | `ruleset_regime_checks.csv` | dual-top non-doubles = `6-5` | supported |
| Four-plus doubles are a narrow candidate gate, not enough by themselves. | `hand_regime_proxy_slices.csv` | 51,108 / 1,184,040 hands = 4.316% | context-limited |
| High-double context matters inside the four-plus-double bucket. | `hand_regime_proxy_slices.csv` | 41.541% miss `6-6`; 69.660% miss at least one of `6-6`/`5-5` | context-limited |
| Off-count liability remains common even in candidate doubles-trump hands. | `hand_regime_proxy_slices.csv` | 8,960 / 51,108 = 17.532% carry at least 10 non-double count points | context-limited |
| No-trump support doubles are measurable as a static hand proxy. | `hand_regime_proxy_slices.csv` | 527,559 / 1,184,040 = 44.556% meet support proxy | underpowered |
| A same-hand no-trump-over-doubles-trump proxy bucket exists, but does not prove the strategy recommendation. | `hand_regime_proxy_slices.csv` | 21,884 / 51,108 = 42.819% of 4+ double hands | underpowered |
| Oracle/regret slices are not available for this claim in current artifacts. | [[w42-concept-bucket-regret]] and local artifact check | existing v0 artifacts expose broad `global:declaration`, not isolated no-trump/doubles rows | not-yet-tested |

## Caveats

- This is not a forge E[Q] rollout. It validates regime legality and static hand
  proxy prevalence, not make/set EV.
- The existing v0 regret artifacts have no isolated no-trump/doubles rows; the
  broad `global:declaration` bucket cannot validate Chapter 9 regime-switch claims.
- The no-trump support and regime-switch buckets are static hand proxies. They do
  not model first lead, contract margin, partner position, hidden ownership, or count
  actually dumped on trick one.
- The low-double sacrifice, dynamic no-trump suit counter, and no-trump defender
  preservation claims remain untested.
- The blank-suit no-trump predicate follows current forge table orientation, where
  non-double leads use the high pip as led suit.
