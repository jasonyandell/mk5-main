## Summary

The missing-bucket gaps this page's "Next Checks" flagged (setter pounce,
effective walkers, 84, scoring) were filled by
[[w42-setter-defense-claim-validation]], [[w42-84-claim-validation]], and
[[w42-scoring-objective-drift-claim-validation]].

[[w42]] now has a report-only concept-bucket regret slice over the merged raw
public-state baseline and raw-plus-v0-strategy-tags baseline.

- report owner bead: `t42-csw6.14`
- report artifacts: `w42/concept_bucket_regret_report/`
- source comparison: [[w42-raw-public-state-baseline]] and
  [[w42-v0-strategy-tags-baseline]]
- evidence mode: aggregation from existing artifacts, with no fresh training or
  data generation

The headline is narrow but useful: the available v0 proxies show the largest
raw-final to tagged-final regret reductions in partner-donation and count-pressure
windows, followed by double-protection/off-risk and suit-pressure/trump-adjacent
positions. Several requested Winning 42 buckets remain missing because the merged
v0 artifacts do not yet expose setter-pounce, walker/endgame, 84, or scoring
detectors.

## Key Question

Where do the cheap public-state/action-local v0 strategy tags reduce regret by
strategy concept, and which Winning 42 buckets are still missing from the current
artifacts?

eval slice. Tagged best scored 2.000 mean regret and 63.93% match.

## Findings

| finding | evidence | metric | status |
|---|---|---|---|
| v0 tags most helped the high-regret count/donation slice proxies. | `concept_bucket_regret.csv` | donation-window delta -2.433; count-pressure delta -2.431 | underpowered model-slice evidence |
| Off-risk/protection also improved materially. | `concept_bucket_regret.csv` | double-protection delta -1.635 regret, +10.88 match pp | underpowered model-slice evidence |
| Trump-adjacent pressure improved, but the current proxy is too broad for a trump claim. | `concept_bucket_regret.csv` | suit-pressure delta -1.090 regret | broad proxy only |
| No-trump/doubles and scoring are not yet claim-testable from these artifacts. | `tag_schema.json`; `bucket_metrics.csv` | declaration group is not regime-isolated; scoring tags absent | missing detector evidence |
| Setter-pounce, 84, and walker/endgame buckets are designed in v1 but not measured in the v0 run. | `strategy_tags_v1_map/detector_map.json` | no v0 bucket metrics for these concepts | not-yet-tested |

and several as missing detectors for follow-up.

## Caveats

- The report reuses a single-seed 560-decision eval slice; it is not a
  confidence interval or multi-seed result.
- Buckets are diagnostic activation slices, not causal ablations.
- The raw comparison is raw final versus tagged final; tagged best is recorded
  separately because it is selected by epoch-level eval regret.
- Several requested strategy concepts are only present in the v1 detector map,
  not in the merged v0 run artifacts.
- No fresh W&B, HF, game generation, model training, or claim-ledger update was
  performed for this bead.
