## What is poured

Positions with their splats, as every pour before it ([[scenario-player]] § the atlas): one
contract (seat 0 bids 30), the planner at every unforced decision of every seat, the belief and
the per-world outcome of every legal move kept. New in this pour:

- **All nine playable declarations.** The bidder's declaring rule is the book's menu
  (`arena.hand_metrics.menu_choice` with no minimum trump length, #155): below four doubles
  exactly the earlier pours' pip bidder, so the pip deals keep their old declarations; from four,
  doubles trump when the pip trump leaves a high off, no trump with no 6-6 and no high off.
  The book's rule fires rarely, so a **coverage floor**: on deals where the book declares a pip,
  a seeded coin forces declaration 7 or 9 on **one deal in eight** (half each). Forced deals are
  listed in the manifest (`forced_deals`), so training can weight or exclude them; the book's own
  declarations are unlabelled. The pour card counts both.
- **Two fields.** Pip deals are planned as before, `walt:k0,n512,F=margin_d250k_h128x64` (the
  shipped v2 net as the field). Doubles-trump and no-trump deals have no net yet, so their field
  is the one-rung Walt, `walt:k1,n512,m8`, exactly what `@jasonyandell/zeb` does for those games.
  Each deal's spec is recorded; a position row knows which field planned it.
- **Chunks.** 500 chunks of 500 deals (seeds 400001 + 500·i), each one `scripts/cpu-slot`
  call (`python -u -m lem.pour.a chunk`, `lem/pour/a.py`, `--threads 4`) with its own `chunk.json`
  and two pour manifests. Sized twice: the smoke chunk (200 deals, seed 9000001, not part of the
  pour) took 26.5 s alone, the doubles/no-trump deals about four times a pip deal; a first launch
  at 1,250 deals on six workers overran the 300 s cap on a machine at load 50 and was discarded
  before any chunk finished (no data kept), so the run is 500 deals on four workers; a chunk is never longer than its slot allows, and a killed chunk is
  re-run from its seed. Deterministic budgets throughout (FAIR CLOCKS).

## Pre-registration

- Size: 250,000 deals. Expected positions about 4.3M (the 250k pour had 4,343,426).
- Coverage targets, counted in the card: the book's own doubles/no-trump declarations
  (unforced) and the forced share; predicted unforced share below 2% of deals (the four-doubles
  condition is rare), forced share 12.5% ± 0.3.
- Nothing is measured against the book here; the pour is data. Its first consumers are the
  first `field-v3` net (trained on it; its audit by the probe kit is the next stage-1 follow-up)
  and Inez's Chapter 9 field.
- Price: by the 2026-10-11 pours, 92 minutes per 50,000 deals per process on this Mac
  (`scratch/atlas/zeb200k/*.log`, 5,512 s for 50,000 deals); the no-net chunks are costlier per
  deal (a one-rung field); estimate about 11 process-hours by the smoke chunk (0.16 s a deal), about three hours of
  wall over six workers under load-based admission. $0.
- Data: a new public CC0 dataset repo (name to the Mayor), with `positions`, `splats` and
  `deals` configs as `scenario.hf publish` lays them out, plus the raw chunks.
