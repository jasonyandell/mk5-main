**STRATEGY-FUSION FENCE.** The 80-draw reader beat the 100-world root-reuse [[the-demon]] **1,075–973** in complete Texas 42 games to seven marks on `marks7-bid30`. Both sides used the same `Bid30Bidder`. First seat after the dealer bids 30, everyone else passes, and the bidder declares the strongest pip according to the common seven-tile hand evaluator. Each improvement in the game score therefore came through tile play.

## Match and cost

The incumbent was the registered `d48909c4` native player in its source-verified `ecd610b7` tournament referee. Development used 96 duplicate pairs and finished 103–89. We froze the 80-draw candidate and the acceptance rule before the independent 1,024-pair test. Each pair repeated the deal stream with the partnerships swapped. One observation for uncertainty is a complete duplicate pair. The work comparison divides total reader instructions by total incumbent instructions across the same games. [Preregistration](https://github.com/jasonyandell/mk5-main/issues/266#issuecomment-6966319203).

The reader swept both games in 142 pairs. The incumbent swept 91, and 791 pairs split.

The preregistered win-rate floor was above 50% and the instruction-ratio ceiling was at most 100%. The intervals use 40,000 whole-pair bootstrap resamples. The player meter covers the entire native process plus the Python seat's startup, choices and shutdown. Artifact builds and the common referee are outside both seats' budgets. Each partnership averaged 17.22 billion instructions for the reader and 21.72 billion for the incumbent per game. On the Apple M5 Max, mean player CPU was 1.17 versus 1.47 seconds and shared elapsed time was 2.65 seconds per game. All runs used deterministic budgets through `scripts/cpu-slot`.

## What the reader sees

The issuing seat supplies its remaining tiles, the public declaration and the plays so far. The completion-count DP from [[demon-power-2026-10-14]] supplies distinct legal candidate deals. For another seat's declaration, the reader reconstructs the bidder's original hand in each deal and checks whether the fixed bidder would name the observed pip. The declaring seat already knows its own hand.

For each previous public play by any other seat, the reader reconstructs that seat's alternatives **at the time of play** in the proposed deal. It assigns positive weights for features including winning count, preserving winners and leading top tiles. The likelihood for the observed move is its weight divided by the weights of all its legal choices. A forced move has likelihood one. The reader weights deals by the square root of the product of those choice likelihoods. These preferences are hand-written.

The reader proposes up to 960 deals for a non-bidder or 320 for the bidder. If the finite pool contains no declaration match, the reader uses public plays to weight that pool. It resamples 80 times, combines identical draws, then uses the original Silas make/set search and margin tiebreak. A bidder on opening lead samples uniformly. Once the mark is settled, the reader plays its lowest legal tile. The instruction results cover the entire policy, including sampling, inference, search and those early exits. The experiment measures their combined effect.

## Playing and replaying it

Run `python -u -m tournament add-demon-bid30` to seed the reader and its compatible reference. The reference carries the original native pin and a separate ID for this referee version. A worker can seat these two pins directly.

```sh
scripts/cpu-slot --threads 1 -- python -u -m tournament work --ladder marks7-bid30 \
  --pairs 1 --serial --max-faceoffs 1 --only \
  demon-drop30-reader-n80-e946b151-8b408c91,demon-pimc-n100-bid9dee8eeb-straight-bounds-support-bait40-d48909c4-bid30-8b408c91
```

Rebuilding both players from their pinned trees and running them through the normal tournament seat reproduced every auction, all 638,260 tile plays, and all 2,048 scores. The release used 79.20% of the incumbent's native instructions (95% interval 78.55–79.85%). The 1,099 distinct development deal seeds and 12,012 distinct confirmation deal seeds had zero overlap. The original registered participant, the new pins, complete games, cost counters, analysis and reproduction commands are in the [public evidence](https://huggingface.co/datasets/jasonyandell/texas-42-zeb-research-2026-11/tree/eafc9036ba0ce501b683843d59c5a91a7609e204/demon/2026-11-17-drop30). The strength estimate applies to this head-to-head and this declaration rule.
