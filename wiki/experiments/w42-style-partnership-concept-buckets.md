## Summary

[[w42]] now has a style/partnership bucket design and a small report-artifact
pilot for the Chapter 14/15 population-style surface.

- report owner bead: `t42-csw6.25`
- report artifacts: `w42/style_partnership_concept_buckets/`
- W&B run: `https://wandb.ai/jasonyandell-forge42/w42/runs/7z6aw6bp`
- evidence mode: report-artifact pilot over existing w42 rich-tag,
  bidding-risk, and partner-support artifacts
- HF links: not applicable

The headline is conservative: overbid restraint and partner legibility have
partial proxy evidence in existing artifacts, while partner-fit residual and
social-pressure robustness need new paired cohorts/wrappers before they are
measurable. No stable player style or partnership quality should be inferred from
a single generated hand.

## Key Question

Which Chapter 14/15 style and partnership concepts can be measured now without
smuggling in private information, and which must stay design-only until w42 has
repeated identities, synthetic style-policy cohorts, bidding counterfactuals, or
paired social-pressure prompts?

## Caveats

- This is a report-artifact pilot, not new model validation.
- Existing numeric slices are single-seed and inherit the 560-decision rich-tag
  eval slice.
- Partner-support rows are underpowered and often unpaired.
- Current artifacts contain decisions, not repeated player/policy identities,
  so partner fit cannot be estimated.
- Social-pressure robustness needs paired prompt or time-budget conditions; it
  cannot be inferred from ordinary state buckets.
- Public-state features may encode legal evidence and priors only. Hidden partner
  needs, true future overtrumps, and team residuals are report-only labels.
