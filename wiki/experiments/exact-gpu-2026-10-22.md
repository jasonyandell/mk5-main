## Question

This is the Metal session of the exact-player track (Exact-GPU, 2026-10-22), beside Exact-1
([[exact-player-s0-2026-10-22]]) and Exact-T1, the trick-1 focal-horizon ladder in
`silas/src/player/trick1.rs`. Can the exact computations run on the GPU with no change to
their arithmetic, equal to their CPU twins bit for bit? Can they then carry Exact-T1's ladder
far enough into trick 1 that its rungs terminate? Rust and Metal only, exact integers, nothing
trains.

## Built (`silas-metal/`, a standalone crate on `silas`)

- **The field epoch** (`shaders/epoch.metal`, `src/epoch.rs`): the fixed field runs in bulk from
  a focal action until the focal seat acts again, with U256 masses at scale 840 per field play.
  A CPU mirror and the Silas reference are its twins.
- **The net** (`src/net.rs`): a level-synchronous solve; Metal expands, and the host merges by
  the full public record. H is the lawful solve (sum, then max). C splits by world, which is the
  fusion itself. STRATEGY-FUSION FENCE: C is the sanctioned negative control and never a value
  ([[strategy-fusion-fence]]).
- **The trick-1 gather** (`shaders/ladder.metal`, `src/ladder.rs`, `src/bin/ladder.rs`), with
  one GPU thread per world:
  - Worlds are unranked in Exact-T1's order, Exact-T1's DFS is walked to depth k+1 viewer
    decisions, and leaves are added into a device hash table.
  - The host drains the table into one sorted run of (path, leaf).
  - The output is Exact-T1's `Gathered` bytes, which its `--load` reads unchanged.
  - The upper column is its clairvoyant bound that refutes (fenced). The rung never chooses by
    it.
- **k = 2 by root tile**: a mask on the legal tiles at depth 0. A tile's gather is exactly the
  whole gather's leaves under that tile, and the rung joins the tiles.
- **Refusals, never crashes**: a leaf budget, a free-memory floor checked before a piece and
  after each drain, and the play budget B2 per root.
- **Receipts**: each piece's bytes are streamed through SHA-256 and FNV-64.

Decisions: `scratch/desks/family-2026-10-22/design-gpu.md`.

## Gates (`cargo test --release` in `silas-metal/`, about 10 s)

- **Epoch parity**: Metal, the CPU mirror and the Silas reference agree on 96,466 children.
- **M3, the h8 gate**: carrier P5, S1 bids 30, 1,200 worlds, uniform field, all 4 roots × arms
  Tricks and Makes30. The Metal net, its CPU mirror and an independent recursive oracle are
  equal bit for bit; H and C equal texas-42's AUDIT; C > H on every root. The test keeps root
  31; the full receipt is `cargo run --release --bin m3` (86.7 s).
- **Ladder parity**: the gather equals Exact-T1's CPU probe files byte for byte, Work included,
  at k = 0 and k = 1 (deal 0, shards 0..1000). Per-tile gathers partition the whole gather
  exactly. A 2^15-slot table, drained many times, equals a 2^20-slot table.
- **k = 2 parity** (the gate Exact-T1's amendment 5 sets): deal 0, tile 6-5, Metal equals
  Exact-T1's CPU pieces, shards 0..14535 (14,500,424 leaves) and 14535..29070 (10,066,997
  leaves), byte for byte.

## Results
