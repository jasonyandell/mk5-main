## Question

A follow-up to #120. [[tie-margin-2026-10-14]] broke Zeb's *exact* ties of the make estimate by
the plan's mean margin. Against exact-tie Zeb it cost no marks (+0.01 [−0.04, +0.07]) and won
55.2% of games to 250. [[book-investigation-2026-10-14]] found one replicated book win: Zeb
breaking ties by the book's rules within 0.02 of its best make probability, a near-tie band,
won 53.2% [51.1, 55.2] of games to 250. At exact ties the same rules won 51.0%. So the band
looked worth about two points of game share there. Does the same widening help margin? The
rule under test accepts any tile within δ of the best make probability, then takes the best
mean margin. Does it beat exact-tie make-then-margin in games to 250 without costing marks?
δ = 0 is the current rule.

## Summary

**No: the band at δ\* = 0.01 does not beat exact-tie make-then-margin.** Both pre-registered bars
miss. In the points game it wins 49.1% [47.7, 50.4] of games to 250 against exact ties. In marks
it is +0.02 [−0.050, +0.086] a game, so its lower bound misses −0.05 by 0.0001. It costs no
measurable marks and buys about half a raw point a hand (+0.41 to +0.52), which mostly does not
survive the book's settlement (+0.13 [−0.17, +0.42]). The A/A arm is exact. Exploratory: the
0.02 band, the book investigation's, reads 53.4% [50.3, 56.5] of games to 250 and +1.18 points
a hand. That came from 384 games, so it went to a registered replication on fresh seeds at the
primary's size (§ Replication). **The replication fails both bars:** 51.4% [49.9, 52.9] of games
to 250, and marks −0.01 [−0.085, +0.075] a game. Its interval sits below the exploratory 53.4%,
the winner's curse the registration named. The band is closed: exact ties stand. No default
moves.

## What δ means

At a decision, q(t) is the make probability that Zeb's plan estimates for tile t. For deployed
Zeb it is estimated on 512 sampled worlds, or the whole fiber when that is smaller. The band
puts every tile with q(t) ≥ q* − δ in the tied set (q ≤ q* + δ for a setter). The plan's mean
margin then picks among them, as in [[tie-margin-2026-10-14]]. So δ is the most estimated make
probability a decision will give up for count.

δ also absorbs sampling noise. Two tiles' q are estimated on the same 512 worlds, and they differ
only where the tiles' outcomes differ. One world is 1/512 ≈ 0.002, so a gap of a few worlds is
mostly sample noise. Re-scored by a stronger copy of itself, Zeb's own pick costs 0.005–0.016
make probability per decision ([[book-study-2026-10-12]] § The face-off). Below about 0.015
the plan cannot tell its best tile from its second. A band inside that noise gives up little true
make probability, and it lets margin decide where make cannot.

The grid, chosen before any measurement:

| δ | worlds of 512 | reading |
|---|---|---|
| 0.005 | 2 | barely past the exact tie: the smallest band that differs from δ = 0 at n = 512 |
| **0.01 (δ\*)** | **5** | **the middle of Zeb's own re-plan noise (0.005–0.016)** |
| 0.02 | 10 | the book investigation's band, at the top of the noise |

**δ\* = 0.01 is the primary.** It sits inside the plan's measured noise, so the make probability it
gives up should mostly be noise rather than a real loss. That keeps the marks bar reachable. It
is wide enough to change the tile at positions the exact rule leaves alone. The 0.02 band won
for the book, but sits at the top of the noise. Below that edge, a real make edge is more likely
to be traded away, so 0.02 is exploratory. So is 0.005, which is close to the exact rule.

## Implementation

`Config.tie_band` (Python referee) and `Tie::Margin { band }` (Rust core) hold δ. The default is
0, it needs `tie="margin"`, and it must lie in [0, 1]. The arena spec is `t=margin:<band>`, and
`t=margin` still means δ = 0. The tied set is `q ≥ q* − δ` for the bidding side and `q ≤ q* + δ`
for a setter, compared as doubles in the same order in both engines. At δ = 0 this is exactly the
old set, because q* is the extreme. Both engines are bit-identical (`scenario/tests/test_tie.py`:
tile, q, nodes and analyses, a batch, a modeled rung's band). The band's tile is the one an
independent reading of the analysis names. δ = 0 decides as `t=margin`. The zeb crate keeps
`Tie::Lowest` and is unchanged, as is Zeb 0.2.0.

## Pre-registration (committed before any measurement)

**The players.** Deployed Zeb, `walt:k0,n512,F=margin_d250k_h128x64,f=16` (compiled, seed 0),
with `t=margin:δ` as challenger A and `t=margin` (exact ties) as B. The field net is
`margin_d250k_h128x64.npz`, the one [[tie-margin-2026-10-14]] graded.

**The arena.** As in [[tie-margin-2026-10-14]] § Step 2. Both sides bid `bid30` (the hub's
protocol). Duplicate deals: each deal seed is played in both orientations. Batches have a
fixed half (games per orientation) and run under `timeout 300`. A batch the timeout kills
writes nothing and is retried at half the size. A cluster is one game index of one batch,
both orientations. Intervals are 95% percentile bootstraps over clusters (10,000 resamples,
`report_grade.py`, unchanged from tie-margin's). Batch lines print throughput only, and no
block is read until its last batch is in.

**Primary: δ\* = 0.01 against exact ties.** Two endpoints, both required:
- **P1, marks (non-inferiority).** Games to 7 marks, at least 1,536 games (base seeds 1001, 1002,
  … in order until the count is reached). The bar is a marks/game interval with lower bound
  above **−0.05**.
- **P2, points (superiority).** Games to 250 points under the book's settlement
  (`ArenaConfig.points_to_win=250`), at least 1,536 games (base seeds 2001, …). The bar is a
  game-share interval with lower bound above **50%**.

**The band beats the exact rule only if both bars clear.** If P1 fails, the band costs marks, or
cannot be shown not to. If P2 fails, it buys nothing measurable in the points game. Either way
it is a finding, and it is reported as measured.

Size: tie-margin's G1 read ±0.055 marks/game at 768 games for two players that part only at
exact ties. A band parts the players somewhat more often. At 1,536 games the expected half-width
is about ±0.04, so a true difference of zero clears −0.05 with about two chances in three. At 768
clusters the game-share half-width is about ±1.2 pp.

**The A/A arm (pre-registered, run first).** A = `t=margin:0`, B = `t=margin`, games to 250, 64
games (base seeds 9101, 9102). Both players are the same rule, so every cluster must be exactly
antisymmetric: marks and points 0, game share 50%, every hand's tallies mirrored. Anything else is
a bug in the band or the harness, and the study stops until it is found.

**Exploratory (no bars).** δ = 0.005 (E005, seeds 3001, …) and δ = 0.02 (E02, seeds 4001, …)
against exact ties, games to 250, at least 384 games each. Also reported beside every block:
marks/game, points per hand (the raw split), the book's settled points per hand, the final score
per game in the points game, made rates, and hands per game.

**Decision.** No default moves and no zeb version changes. If both primary bars clear, this page
proposes δ\* as the band of make-then-margin, should Jason adopt make-then-margin for the next
zeb version ([[tie-margin-2026-10-14]] § Recommendation). Otherwise it reports the miss.

## Results

Deployed Zeb with `t=margin:δ` (A) against deployed Zeb with `t=margin` (B), `bid30` both
sides, duplicate deals, 95% intervals over deal clusters. *Points/hand* is the raw split of a
hand's 42. *Settled* is the book's settlement (made: each side its points; set: the setters
take the bid plus theirs).

| block | δ | game | games (clusters) | marks/game [95%] | game share [95%] | points/hand | settled/hand | final score/game | made, A / B |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| AA | 0 | to 250 | 96 (48) | 0 [0, 0] | 50.0% | 0 | 0 | 0 | 41.4% / 41.4% |
| **P1** | **0.01** | **to 7 marks** | 1,536 (768) | **+0.02 [−0.050, +0.086]** | 49.8% [48.4, 51.1] | +0.52 [+0.39, +0.66] | +0.34 [+0.09, +0.59] | — | 37.1% / 36.9% |
| **P2** | **0.01** | **to 250** | 1,536 (768) | −0.03 [−0.09, +0.03] | **49.1% [47.7, 50.4]** | +0.41 [+0.26, +0.57] | +0.13 [−0.17, +0.42] | +1.1 [−1.4, +3.6] | 38.1% / 38.5% |
| E005 | 0.005 | to 250 | 384 (192) | +0.01 [−0.11, +0.13] | 50.8% [48.2, 53.4] | +0.28 [+0.01, +0.55] | +0.17 [−0.35, +0.70] | +1.5 [−3.1, +6.1] | 39.3% / 39.2% |
| E02 | 0.02 | to 250 | 384 (192) | +0.05 [−0.11, +0.22] | 53.4% [50.3, 56.5] | +1.18 [+0.80, +1.55] | +0.98 [+0.22, +1.76] | +8.4 [+1.8, +15.0] | 36.5% / 36.0% |

- **A/A.** All 48 clusters are exactly antisymmetric (`aa_check.py`): marks, points, wins and
  every hand's tallies mirror. δ = 0 is the exact rule end to end.
- **P1 (marks, non-inferiority): misses, by a hair.** The lower bound is −0.0501 against a bar of
  −0.05. The point estimate is +0.02 and the made rates agree to 0.2 point. Read plainly, the band
  costs no marks that this sample can see, but the registered bar is not cleared.
- **P2 (points, superiority): misses.** 49.1% of games, with the interval below 50.4%. The band
  moves raw points up by under half a point a hand, a tenth of what exact margin ties bought over
  lowest id (+4.55, [[tie-margin-2026-10-14]]). In the book's settlement that is +0.13, and the
  game share does not move.
- **The grid (exploratory).** 0.005 reads flat. 0.02 reads 53.4% of games and +1.18 raw points a
  hand, with the settlement interval above zero. The order 0.005 ≈ 0.01 < 0.02 is not monotone in
  game share (50.8%, 49.1%, 53.4%). With three bands read against a 50% bar, one reading of 53% by chance would not be surprising. A
  pre-registered replication of δ = 0.02 alone, at P2's size, would settle it.
- **A reading, not measured.** How often the band moves the tile was not counted. The likely
  story: exact ties at n = 512 already hand margin the positions where make is truly indifferent,
  mostly decided contracts. A band of 5 worlds adds near ties where make still has a noisy
  preference. There the margin-best tile is plausibly often the make-best tile as well. The book's
  band won against the lowest-id rule, which leaves all the count on the table. Against a rule
  that already plays for count at exact ties, the band has less to add.

**Decision.** Neither primary bar clears, so the band does not beat exact-tie margin, and nothing
is proposed for δ. `Config.tie_band` lands default 0, and Zeb 0.2.0 is unchanged. If Jason adopts
make-then-margin for the next zeb version, exact ties stand as the rule. A δ = 0.02 replication
is the open thread (#120).

**Deviations from the registration, none touching a read.**
- The A/A arm ran 96 games (seeds 9101, 9102, 9104), not 64, because two workers raced.
- P1's first six batches at half 16 overran the 300 s cap and were rerun at half 8, as
  registered. Every P1 batch is at half 8.
- To cap the machine's load at another study's request, seven in-flight batches were stopped and
  rerun (a stopped batch writes nothing). No batch was dropped, and every block holds its seeds in
  order. No block was read before its last batch.

## Replication (δ = 0.02)

The exploratory 0.02 band read 53.4% [50.3, 56.5] of games to 250 on 384 games. This section is
its one registered replication, at the primary's size, on fresh seeds. It is the last chase of
the band: if it fails, the band is closed.

### Pre-registration (committed before any measurement)

**The players.** As in § Pre-registration: deployed Zeb `walt:k0,n512,F=margin_d250k_h128x64,f=16`
(compiled, seed 0; the net's SHA256 `1615989b…5592d`, as published in
`jasonyandell/texas-42-zeb-pour-250k`). Challenger A is `t=margin:0.02`; B is `t=margin` (exact
ties). `bid30` both sides, duplicate deals (each deal seed played in both orientations), a cluster
is one game index of one batch, both orientations. Intervals are 95% percentile bootstraps over
clusters (10,000 resamples), by `report_grade.py` unchanged from the study's.

**Fresh seeds.** Base seeds R1 5001, 5002, …; R2 6001, 6002, …; A/A 9201, 9202, …. These are
disjoint from every base seed the tie-margin study (1–16, 101–124, 201–208, 301–312) and this
page's study (1001–1096, 2001–2048, 3001–3012, 4001–4012, 9101–9104) used. A hand's deal is
`hash((base seed, game, hand, redeal))` (`arena.engine.hand_seed`), so disjoint base seeds give
disjoint seed tuples; `seeds_check.py` also counts 32-bit hand seeds coinciding by hash (0 with
tie-margin, 10 of 1.4 million with tie-band, below chance).

**Two endpoints, both required to pass.**
- **R2, points (superiority, the claim).** Games to 250 under the book's settlement
  (`ArenaConfig.points_to_win=250`), at least 1,536 games, half 8 per batch. The bar: the
  game-share interval's lower bound above **50%**.
- **R1, marks (non-inferiority).** A separate block of games to 7 marks, at least 1,536 games,
  half 4 per batch. The bar: the marks/game interval's lower bound above **−0.05**.

The band replicates only if both bars clear. Anything else is a failed replication, reported as
measured, and the band is closed.

**The A/A arm (run first).** A = `t=margin:0`, B = `t=margin`, games to 250, 64 games (half 8,
base seeds 9201–9204). Every cluster must be exactly antisymmetric (`aa_check.py`). Anything else
stops the study until the cause is found.

**Batches and load.** Another heavy experiment shares the machine, so at most 8 batches run at
once (`sched.py`), and halves are sized to finish well inside the cap (the study's E02 ran
32-game points batches in 70–260 s; R2 runs 16-game batches, R1 8-game ones). Every batch runs
under `timeout 300`. Batch lines print throughput only; no block is read until its last batch is
in.

**The batch-drop rule, fixed now.** A batch the timeout kills (or that fails) writes nothing and
is retried at the same base seed at half the size (8 → 4 → 2 for R2, 4 → 2 for R1). A seed that
is still killed at half 2 is **dropped**: it contributes nothing, it is logged and reported, and
the block takes the next seed in order until it holds at least 1,536 games. A batch stopped by
hand to relieve the machine is not a drop: it wrote nothing and is rerun at the same seed and
size. Drops are decided by the timeout alone, never by outcome, which the batch line does not
show.

**Reported beside the bars, no bars:** points per hand, the book's settled points per hand, the
final score per game (R2), made rates, hands per game, and both blocks pooled with the
exploratory E02 for context only.

**Winner's curse.** The 53.4% was the best of three bands read against a 50% bar, so the
replication's expected share is below it even if the band is real. At 768 clusters the
game-share half-width is about ±1.3 pp: a true 53.4% clears 50% almost surely, a true 51.5%
about half the time.

**Decision.** No default moves and no zeb version changes either way. If both bars clear, this
page proposes δ = 0.02 as make-then-margin's band, should Jason adopt make-then-margin for the
next zeb version. Otherwise the band is closed at δ = 0.

### Replication results

Deployed Zeb with `t=margin:0.02` (A) against deployed Zeb with `t=margin` (B), `bid30` both
sides, duplicate deals, 95% intervals over deal clusters, columns as in § Results.

| block | game | games (clusters) | marks/game [95%] | game share [95%] | points/hand | settled/hand | final score/game | hands/game | made, A / B |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| AA (δ = 0) | to 250 | 64 (32) | 0 [0, 0] | 50.0% | 0 | 0 | 0 | 8.3 | 37.4% / 37.4% |
| **R2** | **to 250** | 1,536 (768) | +0.01 [−0.06, +0.08] | **51.4% [49.9, 52.9]** | +0.51 [+0.32, +0.69] | +0.30 [−0.05, +0.64] | +2.6 [−0.4, +5.5] | 8.5 | 37.8% / 37.7% |
| **R1** | **to 7 marks** | 1,536 (768) | **−0.01 [−0.085, +0.075]** | 50.2% [48.6, 51.8] | +0.65 [+0.49, +0.81] | +0.37 [+0.07, +0.66] | — | 11.2 | 37.3% / 37.4% |

- **A/A.** All 32 clusters are exactly antisymmetric (`aa_check.py`).
- **R2 (points, superiority): fails.** 51.4% of games, with the lower bound at 49.87%, under the
  50% bar.
- **R1 (marks, non-inferiority): fails.** The point estimate is −0.01 and the made rates agree to
  0.1 point, but the lower bound is −0.085, past the −0.05 bar. The band cannot be shown not to
  cost marks.
- **The winner's curse.** The exploratory 53.4% [50.3, 56.5] was the best of three bands read
  against a 50% bar. On fresh seeds and four times the games the same players read 51.4%, and
  the replication's upper bound (52.9%) lies below the exploratory point. The raw points shrank
  the same way: +1.18 a hand in E02, +0.51 here, the size δ = 0.01 bought (+0.41 to +0.52).
  Pooled with E02 for context only (no bar; E02 was selected for its high reading, so the pool
  leans up), R2 reads 51.8% [50.5, 53.1] on 1,920 games (`pooled.py`).
- **What is left.** The band buys about half a raw point a hand over exact ties at both widths
  tried at size, a little of which survives the book's settlement (+0.30 to +0.37). It does not
  move games to 250 by a measurable amount. The book investigation's band won 53.2% against
  the lowest-id rule, which leaves the count on the table; against a rule that already plays
  for count at exact ties, the band's extra count is small.

**Decision.** Both registered bars miss, so the replication fails, and this was the band's last
chase. The band is closed at δ = 0: if Jason adopts make-then-margin for the next zeb version,
exact ties are its rule. `Config.tie_band` stays in the code at default 0, and Zeb 0.2.0 is
unchanged.

**Deviations and drops.** None. No batch was killed, so none was retried and none dropped
(R2's 96 batches ran in 74–198 s and R1's 192 in at most 90 s, at most 8 at once, beside
another heavy experiment). Every block holds its seeds in order (R2 6001–6096, R1 5001–5192,
AA 9201–9204). No block was read before its last batch.
