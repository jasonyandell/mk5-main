## Summary

This report validates a narrow set of odds and ruleset claims from [[winning42-ch01-in-a-nutshell]],
[[winning42-ch13-optional-variations]], and [[winning42-ch16-statistical-odds]] for bead
`t42-csw6.16`.

Epistemic status: confirmed result for exact odds arithmetic and deterministic ruleset
predicates; not a [[forge]] E[Q], [[gus]], w42 model, [[burl]] trace, W&B, or HF result.

Headline: the book/wiki odds and ruleset claims checked here are supported on the tested
slice. Exact enumeration recovers `C(28, 7) = 1,184,040`, the Chapter 16 hand-shape and
double-count priors, the modal two-doubles / one-void bucket, and the four-trump `10/27`
and `14/27` configuration counts. Deterministic predicates also support the Chapter 1
accounting/follow-suit substrate and Chapter 13 straight-ruleset gates checked here.

## Key Question

Can the odds and ruleset claims harvested from Chapters 1, 13, and 16 be validated by exact
enumeration and deterministic checks before any ML modeling?

## Caveats

- `scratch/winning42/winning42.with_figures.md` is absent in this worktree, so the report
  reads the source-slice claims through the chapter wiki pages rather than the OCR source.
- The follow-suit predicate enumerates all declarations, led tiles, and 4-card held subsets,
  not all 7-card hands; this is exact for the predicate shape because only nonempty
  follow-set versus void-set behavior is being checked.
- The optional-variant gate is a deterministic exclusion fixture, not an audit of a generated
  straight-42 corpus.
- The four-trump counts validate assignment arithmetic only. They do not prove that leading
  the double is optimal under bid margin, count pressure, score, or off-protection context.
- No forge E[Q], Gus/w42 model, Burl trace review, W&B run, or HF artifact was used.
