## Question and scope

This retrospective study asks how fixed layer-1/unit-65 (L1/U65) native
preactivation and its sensitivity to a layer-0/head1 current-lead attention
cut change from splat initialization through 200,000 training steps. The unit
was selected from the final model, so the coordinate trajectory is not proof
that one semantic feature persisted through training. This is not an all-unit
clean-share estimate, fresh semantic confirmation, recursive-generalization
test, or playing-strength evaluation.

## Frozen trajectory and inputs

Ten saved seed-42 snapshots were measured: steps 0, 250, 1,000, 5,000,
20,000, 50,000, 100,000, 150,000, 195,000 and 200,000. The 195,000
checkpoint matches the best-model parent. At each checkpoint, the instrument
used the same 48 lawful prefixes as [[gus-route-rescue-2026-10-20]]: 12
matched physical pairs in each of its already-inspected development and
confirmation panels. Ten snapshots were examined: nine newly measured and
step 195,000 reused from its audited records. These panels are reused
diagnostics, not independent new confirmation. The original input, fixed G0
focal/partner and pinned Zeb
opponents stayed unchanged; all 607,306 reserved roots remained excluded.
No new games, roots, training, player search or opponent change occurred.

At each new checkpoint the study retained native baseline pre/post-GELU U65,
raw/legal output fields and layer-0/head1 source attention, plus final-actor
query-only score attenuation at doses 0.25 and 0.5 for the current lead tile
and preceding lead-actor marker. The selected unnormalized attention weight
is multiplied by `1 − dose`, then all weights are renormalized. Equal nominal
doses need not remove equal attention mass. The contrast compares two route
interventions, not equalized information or per-edge importance.

## Registered primary result

For each pair and step, the primary was the positive-metadata-side minus
negative-metadata-side change in cut-minus-baseline raw pre-GELU U65 for
lead-tile attenuation minus the corresponding lead-actor attenuation, at
dose 0.5. The registered endpoint was step 200,000 minus initialization,
with a paired bootstrap over the same 12 physical pairs within each panel.

| Reused panel | Paired endpoint change, raw preactivation units | Descriptive 95% bootstrap interval | Registered direction |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| Development | −1.753963 | [−1.849160, −1.665117] | Supported |
| Confirmation | −1.495585 | [−1.874091, −1.059430] | Supported |

The registered directional prediction was supported in both reused panels.
At initialization the same contrast was near zero (development −0.000596;
confirmation −0.000992); at step 200,000 it was negative in each panel.
These endpoint findings do not imply a monotone curve or identify when the
change occurred. The intermediate checkpoints are descriptive, and the two
panels reuse the prior 48 prefixes.

Native baseline U65 also changed across the trajectory on these contexts:
initially its paired positive-minus-negative metadata-side preactivation
contrast was close to zero in both panels (development +0.000761,
confirmation +0.000321); at step 200,000 it was +3.568995 and +3.325819.
The metadata labels are double-blank versus matched near-miss lead contexts,
not activation poles. This association does not certify a blank-suit concept
or establish same-feature continuity. U65 remains a fixed tensor coordinate;
changes in scale or gain can contribute. The contrast was already visible at
the sampled 1,000-step checkpoint (development +0.772216; confirmation
+0.521929 raw preactivation units), then fluctuated at later checkpoints; the
sampling grid does not locate its onset.

## Audit and evidence

The bounded run completed all 480 units: 432 newly measured checkpoint/row
units and 48 reused step-195,000 units, with 5,616 new forwards. All required
audits passed; every newly measured explicit-functional comparison was
bit-identical to native forward (maximum error 0). No training or player
evaluation was performed.

The frozen protocol and registration were published and anonymously verified
before the first new trained-response forward: [registration at revision
`d9d4fddd2d15ad12376e024239b2f2b8a5b723a0`](https://huggingface.co/datasets/jasonyandell/texas-42-zeb-research-2026-10/tree/d9d4fddd2d15ad12376e024239b2f2b8a5b723a0/lem/gus-u65-formation-2026-10-20/registration).
The [immutable result archive at revision
`dafe0e47f20209542c6bcb753abe61dfa860120d`](https://huggingface.co/datasets/jasonyandell/texas-42-zeb-research-2026-10/tree/dafe0e47f20209542c6bcb753abe61dfa860120d/lem/gus-u65-formation-2026-10-20/evidence)
contains nine files and 6,133 archived members (31,675,836 bytes); fresh
anonymous file/member verification passed. Independent saved-array review
reconstructed all activation and route curves, endpoint bootstrap intervals
and selected cards without additional model forwards.

The same owner-private [First Look report](https://gus-first-look.psychometrix.chatgpt.site/#formation)
now presents the ten saved checkpoints with both reused panels, pre/post-GELU
means, route sensitivity and one fixed full-input example per panel. All 20
selector combinations, checkpoint navigation, input disclosure and 390-pixel
layout were checked. Site source `9335b9e578e2e63055d404a13f85854fa1bf65aa`
deployed successfully; no scientific measurements were repeated for publication.

## Interpretation boundary

The study adds a saved-checkpoint trajectory for one final-selected native
coordinate and a registered, local route-sensitivity contrast. It does not
establish feature identity across checkpoints, causal mediation of output
choices, general clean-neuron formation, a player improvement, or the
essay's broader account of representation growth. See [[gus-native-cooldown-2026-10-19]],
[[gus-first-look-2026-10-19]] and [[gus-route-rescue-2026-10-20]] for the
related formation and current-lead route evidence.
