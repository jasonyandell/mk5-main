## Summary

[[w42]] bead `t42-csw6.18` validates the available bidder-sequencing evidence
for Chapter 3 claims: trump pull, reentry preservation, off timing, count
inventory, and laydown correctness.

The available local artifacts support only a conservative secondary analysis.
They show that existing cheap strategy tags improved oracle-alignment metrics on
sequencing-adjacent buckets, but they do not contain direct paired sequence
counterfactuals, a final-trump/reentry detector, or a deterministic laydown proof
checker. The report therefore leaves the claim ledger unchanged.

## Key Question

Can existing w42 artifacts statistically validate bidder sequencing claims from
[[winning42-ch03-bidder-play]], especially trump pull, reentry preservation, off
timing, count inventory, and laydown correctness?

## Method

| field | value |
|---|---|
| report owner bead | `t42-csw6.18` |
| ruleset / score mode | inherited from Gus/forge corpus artifacts; straight generated-game corpus, no new variant gate |
| evidence mode | secondary analysis of existing w42 oracle-regret/model artifacts |
| decision slice | bidder-play proxies over all available eval decisions, not bidder-seat-only sequence counterfactuals |
| train/eval split policy | `w42-seed-bucket-v1`; eval corpus path records `gus/data/corpus_eval_20.pt`, eval limit `560`, eval seed `43` |
| statistical test | Wilson/Newcombe-style 95% intervals for aggregate match-rate and near-tie deltas; descriptive mean-regret deltas because per-row variance was not retained |
| success criterion | move claim only if direct detector/counterfactual evidence exists; otherwise report proxy support without ledger promotion |

The source eval was paired by row, but the retained metric artifact is bucket
aggregate only. The confidence intervals therefore treat raw/tagged bucket
match rates as independent proportions. This is conservative but loses paired
power.

## Findings

| claim id | status | proxy buckets | weighted delta mean regret | weighted delta match rate | 95% proxy match-delta span | finding |
|---|---|---|---:|---:|---|---|
| `ch03-trump-pull-sequencing` | underpowered | `global:current_trick_pressure`; `action:trick_relation`; `action:suit_pressure` | `-1.017278` | `+0.078806` | `[-0.020447, 0.182711]` | Sequencing-adjacent pressure buckets improve model alignment, but no trump-first/off-first counterfactual was run. |
| `ch03-reentry-preservation` | underpowered | `action:double_protection`; `global:hand_shape`; `action:hand_shape` | `-1.045158` | `+0.082721` | `[-0.043710, 0.269112]` | Available buckets touch protection and shape, not the final-trump-spent-before-off-clear mechanism. |
| `ch03-off-timing` | underpowered | `action:suit_pressure`; `action:double_protection`; `global:void_summary` | `-0.898029` | `+0.078517` | `[-0.043710, 0.269112]` | Off-timing proxies improve alignment, but no paired early-off versus trump-first rollout exists. |
| `ch03-count-inventory` | context-limited | `action:count_pressure`; `global:public_count`; `global:unseen_count_by_pip` | `-1.082058` | `+0.080573` | `[-0.058563, 0.298204]` | Count buckets show the strongest proxy regret gains; this validates tag usefulness, not the full book rule. |
| `ch03-laydown-correctness` | not-yet-tested | not applicable | not applicable | not applicable | not applicable | No deterministic laydown proof checker or late-endgame enumeration artifact exists in the available outputs. |

The strongest single proxy bucket was `action:count_pressure`: mean regret fell
from `5.296599` to `2.908336` on `n=113`, a `-2.388263` delta, with match rate
moving from `0.371681` to `0.495575`. The conservative aggregate 95% interval
for the match-rate delta spans `[-0.058563, 0.298204]`.

## Caveats

- The source artifacts are aggregate bucket metrics, not row-level paired
  predictions for every eval decision.
- The worktree lacks the original `gus/data` corpora, so no fresh oracle rollout
  or direct detector run was possible.
- Proxy buckets are not bidder-seat-only and do not isolate Chapter 3 sequence
  interventions.
- Mean regret deltas are descriptive because aggregate artifacts do not retain
  per-row variance.
- Laydown correctness needs an exact proof checker; none exists in these outputs.
- `E[Q] N=10` remains far stronger than the tiny tagged model: mean regret
  `0.117969` versus tagged-best `1.999707` on the same `n=560` eval slice.
