## Summary

Issue #161, the arm [[book-small-n-2026-10-14]] stopped short of: the [[book-authority]]'s
measurement of assumption A7 ([[book-assumptions]]) where the book's reader lives. #154 found
the book free as Zeb's near-tie break at 16, 64 and 160 worlds, and its sets' cost falling from
1.10 marks/game at 160 worlds to 0.21 [−0.02, 0.44] at 16. Every #154 fallback still planned.
Here the reader plans over no worlds: where no rule fires, the reader plays their lowest legal tile.

## Results (complete, 2026-10-14; 152 of 152 registered batches ran, none dropped)

**One line.** For the reader the book writes for, one who plans over no worlds, the book's rules
are worth +2.47 [2.28, 2.65] marks/game (288 game pairs; A/A −0.04 [−0.15, +0.06]). Planning
nothing costs 4.03 [3.84, 4.23] marks/game against a 16-world plan; the rules recover 2.47 of it,
about 61%. At 16 worlds the same rules as a filter are worth −0.10 [−0.34, +0.13], so the rules'
value rises by 2.57 [2.32, 2.83] marks/game as planning goes to nothing: the book is worth the most
exactly where its reader lives, as A7 holds.

Data: [texas-42-zeb-research-2026-10](https://huggingface.co/datasets/jasonyandell/texas-42-zeb-research-2026-10)
`book/no-plan/` (per batch `meta.json` and `games.jsonl`, `summary.json`, the queue log, `CARD.md`).

### Reading, under [[book-authority]]

- **Assumptions held and violated.** A7 is restored where it matters: the reader plans over no
  worlds, and the book is the reader's only policy above the lowest tile. A1, A2, A3, A4 and A6 are violated
  as in every arena block; the marks arms violate A5 and the 250-point arms restore it. The reader
  is still not the book's: a person at the table brings judgment the book assumes and does not
  write down (A8), where this one brings the lowest tile.
- **What our model can see.** The rules take a reader who makes 21% of their contracts to 44%, and
  hold the planner opposite from 78% to 69%. They are worth 2.47 marks a game to the reader, and about 61%
  of what a 16-world plan is worth (2.47 of 4.03; the two intervals are above, no interval on the
  ratio). The trend from #154 completes: worth −0.10 inside a 16-world plan, +2.47 to a reader with
  none.
- **What it cannot yet see.** The book's reader against the book's own readers: here the opponent
  is deployed Zeb, a planner that reads nothing across hands and does not read the book (A1, A2).
  The 1.56 marks a game K0 still gives up to a 16-world plan is the scope of that table, the book
  written down literally (A8), and the fallback below the rules.
- **The measurement next.** The same reader with the book's partner reading (#150's reader at the
  fallback; A2), and a reader whose fallback is a person's, not the lowest tile: for the first, a
  re-run of K0 with `read=`; for the second, no arm yet.

## Design (pre-registered before any measured batch; see `scenario/no_plan.py`)

`scenario/no_plan.py` holds the registration in its docstring; the batch runner it shares with
the desk's later studies is `scenario/book_batches.py`.

- **Players.** Team B is deployed Zeb 0.3.0 ([[zeb-030-confirmation-2026-10-14]]); both teams bid
  by the heuristic rule at caution 0. Team A: **L0** the lowest legal tile, always; **K0** the book
  player over the same fallback (the head-to-head's variant (a), [[book-head-to-head-2026-10-13]]),
  so K0 and L0 differ only by the rules; **A0** L0 against Zeb at planner seed 1, the A/A; **Z16**
  and **F16**, #154's 16-world arms on these seeds.
- **Arms.** Marks: L0, K0, A0, Z16, F16. The book's game (250 points): PL0, PK0.
- **Estimator.** As [[book-small-n-2026-10-14]]: game pairs, per-pair contrasts, 95% percentile
  bootstrap (10,000 resamples, seed 42).
- **Primary.** D0 = K0 − L0 marks/game; **the book predicts D0 > 0.** The timing pilot already
  puts it far above 0 (+2.9 on 4 excluded pairs: L0 makes 18% of its contracts, K0 42%), so the
  primary is expected to clear. What the study can still learn is in the secondaries.
- **Secondaries.** The trend from 16 worlds to none, D0 − E16 with E16 = F16 − Z16 (the book
  predicts above 0); the cost of planning nothing, L0 − Z16, and how much of it the rules recover,
  K0 − Z16; the book's game, PK0 − PL0 on games won and settled points; mechanism by arm.
- **Size.** 288 game pairs per marks arm (seeds 2101–2124 × 12), 192 per points arm (2201–2216 ×
  12): MDE 0.45 on the trend at SD 2.7, 0.36 on the primary at SD 2.2. 152 batches, two at a time
  through `scripts/cpu-slot`, seed-major.
- **Batch-drop rule and fence.** As [[book-small-n-2026-10-14]].
