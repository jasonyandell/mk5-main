## Summary

Issue #161, the arm [[book-small-n-2026-10-14]] stopped short of: the [[book-authority]]'s
measurement of assumption A7 ([[book-assumptions]]) where the book's reader lives. #154 found
the book free as Zeb's near-tie break at 16, 64 and 160 worlds, and its sets' cost falling from
1.10 marks/game at 160 worlds to 0.21 [−0.02, 0.44] at 16. Every #154 fallback still planned.
Here the reader plans over no worlds: where no rule fires he plays his lowest legal tile.

*Results follow once every registered batch has run.*

## Design (pre-registered before any measured batch; see `scenario/no_plan.py`)

`scenario/no_plan.py` holds the registration in its docstring; the batch runner it shares with
the desk's later studies is `scenario/book_batches.py`.

- **Players.** Team B is deployed Zeb 0.3.0 ([[zeb-030-confirmation-2026-10-14]]); both teams bid
  by the heuristic rule at caution 0. Team A: **L0** the lowest legal tile, always; **K0** the book
  player over the same fallback (the head-to-head's variant (a), [[book-head-to-head-2026-10-13]]),
  so K0 and L0 differ only by the rules; **A0** L0 against Zeb at planner seed 1, the A/A; **Z16**
  and **F16**, #154's 16-world arms on these seeds.
- **Arms.** Marks: L0, K0, A0, Z16, F16. The book's game (250 points): PL0, PK0.
- **Estimator.** As [[book-small-n-2026-10-14]]: game pairs, per-pair contrasts, 95% percentile
  bootstrap (10,000 resamples, seed 42).
- **Primary.** D0 = K0 − L0 marks/game; **the book predicts D0 > 0.** The timing pilot already
  puts it far above 0 (+2.9 on 4 excluded pairs: L0 makes 18% of its contracts, K0 42%), so the
  primary is expected to clear. What the study can still learn is in the secondaries.
- **Secondaries.** The trend from 16 worlds to none, D0 − E16 with E16 = F16 − Z16 (the book
  predicts above 0); the cost of planning nothing, L0 − Z16, and how much of it the rules recover,
  K0 − Z16; the book's game, PK0 − PL0 on games won and settled points; mechanism by arm.
- **Size.** 288 game pairs per marks arm (seeds 2101–2124 × 12), 192 per points arm (2201–2216 ×
  12): MDE 0.45 on the trend at SD 2.7, 0.36 on the primary at SD 2.2. 152 batches, two at a time
  through `scripts/cpu-slot`, seed-major.
- **Batch-drop rule and fence.** As [[book-small-n-2026-10-14]].
