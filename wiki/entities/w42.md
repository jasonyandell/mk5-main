## Summary

w42 is the Winning 42 empirical strategy-validation workstream. It is a fresh
model/research line for testing whether human-legible strategy concepts from the
book harvest can become measurable detectors, report buckets, training features,
and small-model inputs.

w42 treats the book as a source of hypotheses, not as ground truth. Its job is to
turn claims like "bid only enough," "donate count safely," "double ahead of your
off," "create a pounce window," and "preserve the 84 stopper" into things the
project can count, slice, test, falsify, and explain.

The initial survey is summarized in [[w42-final-empirical-strategy-report]].
Its current decision is [[w42-promote-or-retire]]: keep w42 active as research,
promote the top-level `w42/` research home plus the reporting/W&B/substrate-check
discipline, and do not promote research models into Gus or Burl yet.

## Why Not Gus

[[gus]] remains the oracle-distillation sibling: a multi-head student trained
against [[forge]] E[Q] labels, with belief, value, policy, and Q heads. Gus can
host probes, and the strategy-tags probe showed real signal, but w42 should not
turn Gus into a mixed-purpose research workbench.

w42 is allowed to be smaller, messier, faster to retrain, and more report-shaped.
It can fail without contaminating Gus's core path. If w42 earns promotion later,
that promotion should be explicit and should name which detectors, features,
reports, or model components move into Gus, Burl, or forge-facing infrastructure.

Gus core model and training paths are out of scope for w42 unless a later
promotion bead explicitly approves the change.

## Owns / Borrows / Does Not Touch

Owns:

- the w42 charter, wiki pages, report conventions, and claim ledger
- strategy-concept detector definitions and detector-readiness notes
- small w42 model experiments that compare raw public state, cheap tags, rich
  strategy tags, and concept-bucket inputs
- concept-bucket reporting for book claims, regret, tail risk, belief quality,
  and sample efficiency
- W&B/HF naming conventions once training or durable datasets begin
- the promote / continue / retire decision for the w42 workstream

Borrows:

- [[forge]] E[Q] labels, regret evaluation, enumeration checks, and perfect-info
  oracle caveats
- [[gus]] corpus formats, public-state encodings, and the strategy-tags probe
  lessons
- [[forge-analysis]] report discipline: explicit epistemic status, tables,
  figures, caveats, and reproducible artifacts
- [[winning42-strategy-measurement]] and the chapter pages as the hypothesis
  inventory
- [[burl]] trace review and tool-call concepts where a later bead asks whether
  a strategy concept is visible in reasoning or post-commit explanation

Does not touch:

- Gus core training defaults, model architecture, or production eval baselines
  without a later promotion bead
- forge oracle semantics or E[Q] definitions
- Burl harness behavior, chat behavior, or tool schemas unless a specific w42
  bead scopes that integration
- book claims as hard-coded rules of play
- private or illegal table-talk information that is not available in public
  game state

## Data Sources

w42 should start with generated-game and oracle artifacts already understood by
the project:

- forge enumeration and E[Q] outputs for claim checking and regret labels
- Gus public-state corpora and strategy-probe feature shapes where appropriate
- Winning 42 chapter pages for detector hypotheses and source context
- Burl traces only when the question is about reasoning, explanation, tool use,
  or post-commit conversation

The first data principle is to separate "can this claim be measured?" from "does
this feature improve a model?" Many book claims should first become detectors and
tables before they become model inputs.

## Model Boundary

w42 may train cheap supervised probes from its own top-level research home.
Training is expected to take minutes; data generation is expected to be the
slower and more valuable artifact.

The default comparisons should be:

- raw public state only
- raw public state plus the existing cheap strategy tags
- raw public state plus richer chapter-derived tags
- ablations by concept bucket, especially bidding risk, count donation, trump
  pressure, off protection, pounce windows, 84 preservation, and endgame walkers

`E[Q] N=10` is the current boss baseline for cheap inference. w42 does not need
to beat it immediately to be useful. A result is valuable if it exposes which
strategy concepts are measurable, which help sample efficiency, which reduce
tail-risk blunders, and which remain noisy or unhelpful.

The E[Q] PDF visualizer exposed an important refinement: strategy evaluation
should not collapse every decision to a scalar expected value. A play with a
moderate mean and high variance may really be a branch-shaped state: one shelf of
safe futures, one disaster tail, and a tactical need to brace, preserve a stopper,
or choose a mitigating line. Future w42 probes should therefore treat distribution
shape as first-class evidence where available: make/set threshold mass, variance,
quantiles, lower-tail risk, branch labels, and mitigation tags should sit beside
mean regret rather than being hidden behind it.

w42 also has access to something no human player has directly: generated sampled
worlds where hidden domino ownership and outcome branches are recorded together.
That makes it possible to estimate belief impact magnitude. Even if the live
agent cannot know who holds a hidden domino, offline reports can ask which
specific unseen dominoes, holders, or positions drive the high-outcome shelf, the
disaster shelf, or the middle lumps in an E[Q] PDF. These labels should be used
as diagnostic and training targets for belief quality, threat attribution, and
mitigation analysis, while preserving the boundary that live features must come
only from legal public state or learned beliefs.

The current run-comparison surface is [[w42-wandb-run-comparison-dashboard]]:
raw/v0 full baselines remain local-only, while rich-tag and claim-validation
runs are live in W&B with direct links for primary, superseded, diagnostic, and
failed attempts.

The current next-model decision is [[w42-next-model-decision]]: build a targeted
v2 direct-detector probe for one tactical regime, with distribution-aware labels
where possible, before expanding model capacity or pushing w42 features into Gus.
The first bridge artifact for that work is [[w42-phase2-decision-table]], a
140-decision / 346-action table that joins E[Q] PDFs to seat/role context and
explicitly marks the missing bid-margin and hidden-ownership columns.

The first powered joint-world follow-up is [[w42-powered-branch-atlas-v1]]. It
generates two N=1000 schema-v2 E[Q] games with saved `world_hands` and
`q_per_world`, builds 56 decision rows, 134 action rows, and 1026 hidden-threat
rows, and logs a live W&B dashboard with repeated progress points over processed
decisions. This moves hidden-domino impact from schema proposal to empirical
pilot while keeping the claim ledger unchanged.

[[w42-branch-atlas-scaled-v0]] expands that loop to one seed across all ten
declarations: 280 decisions, 773 legal-action rows, and 5955 hidden-threat rows.
It also makes the E[Q] generator and atlas bid-aware for threshold computation.
The scaled v0 artifact still uses fixed `bid_value=30`; real bid margin and
"bid only enough" claims remain untested.

[[w42-gus-corpus-tactical-claim-deep-dive]] uses the existing Gus v2
all-declaration joint-world corpus instead of regenerating seeds. It processes
28,000 decisions and 75,079 legal actions, producing direct evidence for
role-gated setter pounce and partner donation labels. On pip declarations, best
setter pounce-count actions beat same-decision alternatives by about +4.1 Q, and
reckless count into the bidder side is about -8.4 Q versus non-reckless actions.
The result supports those operationalized tactical labels on the saved Gus v2
slice; it does not validate all book advice or real bid-margin play.

[[w42-phase2-claim-analysis-matrix]] routes the 64 current claim-ledger rows
into testability classes, required fields, leakage risks, sample/power needs,
target wiki pages, and next beads. It is a planning artifact, not a claim-status
movement.

[[w42-claim-data-inventory]] inventories the local Gus corpus after the W42
session recovery found the 111 GB data pile under `gus/data/`. It confirms 100
legacy seed-modulo chunks, 10 schema-v2 fixed-bid all-declaration files, 280000
legacy decisions, and a revised claim-family route table. The inventory changes
planning, not claim statuses: hidden-threat diagnostics and Chapter 9
within-regime tactical labels can use the big corpus now, while hard bid-margin
and true 84-contract claims still need generated data.

[[w42-doubles-no-trump-legacy-mining]] uses that large legacy corpus for the
first Chapter 9 within-regime tactical pass: 56000 declaration-7/9 decisions,
149415 legal action rows, and 25906 proxy-labeled action rows. No-trump double
weapon/control proxies show strong positive action value; broad low-double
sacrifice and `6-5` dual-top proxies need sharper state gates before claim
promotion.

[[w42-hidden-threat-legacy-mining]] uses the same 100 legacy Gus chunks for the
bead `t42-0b4l.4` hidden-threat pass: 280000 decisions and 748305 legal actions
summarized without a giant row dump. It finds that close-mean action choices can
cut lower-tail mass and hidden-downside score while preserving scalar mean,
giving downstream claim families a branch-impact diagnostic surface.

[[w42-phase2-84-weapon-preservation-probe]] now has a dynamic `t42-0b4l.7`
follow-up, not just a static fixture spec. Six explicit bid-84 hands generate
168 decision states, 584 legal action rows, and 4497 hidden-threat rows with
schema-v2 E[Q] PDFs. The run creates proxy labels for live doubles, live
same-suit pairs, pair protectors, dead-asset releases, and 13 same-decision
preserve-vs-spend contrasts. It is targeted evidence for the label surface, not
a central claim-status promotion.

[[w42-bidding-risk-budget-claim-validation]] now has a `t42-0b4l.5`
bid-only-enough follow-up under `w42/bid_only_enough_claim_tests/`. The small
generated probe holds hand and declaration fixed, varies only current high bid
and bid threshold, and emits 1224 bid counterfactual rows plus 1104 positive
margin contrasts. It partially validates the same-contract direction of bidding
only enough, while leaving real auction discipline, partner signals, opponent
responses, and natural bid buckets unpromoted.

[[w42-phase2-seat-position-strategy-map]] now has a `t42-0b4l.6` row-level
claim-test follow-up under `w42/seat_position_claim_tests/`. It consumes the
75,079 full legal-action rows from [[w42-tactical-claim-replication]] and
derives seat, phase, role, partner-support, pounce, and closure labels. Structural
seat and role labels remain slice evidence; action-local pounce/support/closure
labels have same-decision paired contrasts.

[[w42-claim-tag-model-probe]] is the direct `t42-0b4l.9` model-probe follow-up.
It trains a cheap legal-action row model on public/action features, then adds
the claim detector tags from the seat/position artifact. On the held-out seed
split, tags reduce selected mean regret from about `1.48` to `1.32` in the
primary run, with pounce/donation carrying the largest family-drop signal. This
is model-feature evidence, not claim proof.

[[w42-claim-analysis-synthesis-report]] closes the `t42-0b4l` phase-2 recovery
epic and opens `t42-qtwb` for phase-3 generated counterfactual work. The final
state is deliberately conservative: phase 2 produced narrow supports, partial
probes, and diagnostic/model-feature evidence, but no broad book claim is
promoted without a sharper operationalization.

[[w42-phase3-auction-bid-discipline-corpus]] is the first `t42-qtwb` follow-up.
It generates 32 full deals, 384 seat/declaration contract labels, 608 auction
contexts, and 16800 P0 bid-action rows. No positive-margin bid improves over the
minimum winning bid, strengthening the operational bid-only-enough slice under
auction pressure. Natural bid buckets and partner-bid signal remain
context-limited: only 17.708% of contracts land on natural max-profitable
threshold buckets, and P0 overcalls partner in 40.104% of generated partner-high
contexts.

[[w42-phase3-84-seed-mining-corpus]] closes the `t42-qtwb.2` seed-mining route
for 84/endgame work. It scans 50000 seeds across all seats and pip declarations,
emitting 214229 candidate rows and 256 recommended natural seeds for future
branch-atlas or state-injection runs. It is a data-route artifact: it replaces
hand-built-only fixture dependence, but it does not score preserve/spend action
regret by itself.

[[w42-phase3-sequence-seat-counterfactuals]] closes the `t42-qtwb.3`
sequence/seat counterfactual route over the existing 75079 legal-action table.
It emits 14 paired contrasts and 394 slice rows for bidder lead plans,
follow-seat obligations, partner support timing, setter pounce windows, and
last-seat closure. Follow control, closure, setter pounce, and unsafe-count
negative controls are strong; partner support is small-positive and gated; a
pooled called-suit lead over off-suit lead is negative, while called-double over
lower called-suit is strongly positive.

[[w42-phase3-joined-claim-row-model-table]] closes the `t42-qtwb.4` joined-table
route. It normalizes sequence/seat, bidding risk, public 84 bidder-structure,
doubles/no-trump, and hidden public proxy labels into one 75079-row legal-action
table. On a seed-mod held-out split, all public claim-family tags improve the
row model from `1.3598` to `1.1257` mean regret and from `64.464%` to
`68.107%` best-mean match. Dropping sequence/seat removes the gain; bidding and
84 contribute smaller positive signal; hidden public proxy is slightly harmful
as a model feature and remains diagnostic.

[[w42-phase4-sequence-handshape-tests]] closes the first `t42-br7n.1` tactical
proxy lane over 75079 legal actions. Commanding called doubles beat off leads,
generic non-double called-suit leads lose badly, exact closure count donation is
strong, partner count before closure is weak, and setter count-calling/pounce
labels remain robust. Exact private hand-shape claims still need generated state
fields.

[[w42-phase4-84-dynamic-seed-tests]] turns mined natural 84 seeds into a
reached-state branch-atlas artifact. It covers 24 seed games, 672 decisions, and
1872 actions. Defender preserve/spend and dead-asset release proxies are
positive, and bidder trump-pull before final-off is strongly positive; final set
attribution and score 42-vs-84 gates remain blocked.

[[w42-phase4-doubles-notrump-regime-tests]] provides the first paired same-hand
doubles-trump versus no-trump regime test. In the generated policy simulation,
no-trump beats doubles-trump on most four-plus-double hands; high/top double
control is the near-flat pro-doubles slice. The broad "many doubles" rule stays
context-limited.

[[w42-phase4-laydown-rule-accounting]] closes the deterministic rule-accounting
and laydown-proof lane for `t42-br7n.4`. It produces 29 deterministic assertions
with zero failures, supports the Chapter 1 rule substrate, and gives Chapter 3
an executable exact laydown checker.

[[w42-phase4-scoring-objective-tests]] closes the scoring-objective lane for
`t42-br7n.3`. It generates 4000 hand traces, 640 match proxies, and 160 timed
advancement trials. Marks create early terminal states, erase defender partial
points in made ordinary contracts, compress ordinary set severity, and disagree
with point-score winners in about `15.5%` of proxy matches.

[[w42-phase4-claim-completion-board]] is the phase-4 ledger control surface for
`t42-br7n.6`. It represents all 64 book claims, routes 30 to active phase-4
children, marks 25 as complete or closeable under current evidence, identifies 2
ledger-reconciliation candidates, and exposes 7 bidding/count-exposure rows
that now belong to `t42-br7n.7`.

[[w42-phase4-bidding-count-exposure-tests]] closes that `.7` scope gap. It gives
the seven Chapter 2 / Chapter 12 / Chapter 16 rows reproducible
generated-contract and static-detector evidence: three-plus trumps are much
stronger, risk <=12 is modestly better, four/five offs are worse, natural
max-profitable buckets appear in 66 / 384 rows, partner two-plus-double prior is
about `58%`, and double-side protection is explicitly side-limited.

[[w42-phase4-final-claim-audit]] closes the loop around the whole phase-4 sweep.
It confirms all 64 ledger rows have evidence and/or explicit bounded blockers;
no row is ownerless. The audit leaves 39 bounded future-work rows, including 2

## Report and Artifact Conventions

Expected locations:

- wiki narrative and decisions: `wiki/entities/w42.md` and
  `wiki/experiments/w42-*.md`
- durable code, local research scripts, and current artifacts: `w42/`
- temporary notebooks and unrelated one-off experiments: `scratch/`
- durable tables and figures: report paths named by the bead that creates the
  analysis, following the forge-analysis habit of separating tables, figures,
  and narrative summaries
- W&B: training runs, sweeps, and metric panels once a model bead starts
- HuggingFace: durable datasets, model checkpoints, or public artifacts only
  after the data/model card is clear enough to explain what was measured

Every report should state:

- question
- data slice
- detector or feature set
- baselines
- metrics
- claim-ledger impact
- caveats
- exact commands or artifact manifests

## Claim Ledger

w42 should keep the conservative ledger style established by
[[winning42-strategy-measurement]].

Claim statuses:

- `not-yet-tested` - detector or hypothesis exists, but no evidence run has
  checked it
- `supported` - enumeration, oracle rollout, Gus/w42 probe, or Burl trace review
  supports the claim on the stated slice
- `contradicted` - the evidence argues against the claim on the stated slice
- `context-limited` - the claim appears true only in a restricted regime
- `underpowered` - the available sample or proxy is too weak for a verdict

No claim should move out of `not-yet-tested`, `context-limited`, or
`underpowered` just because a detector exists. Detector creation is not empirical
support.

## Promotion / Retirement

Promote w42 outputs when they produce repeatable value beyond local research:

- a detector becomes useful across multiple reports or models
- a feature bucket improves sample efficiency, regret, tail risk, or explanation
  quality on held-out slices
- a report changes a Gus, Burl, or forge-analysis decision
- a dataset/checkpoint is stable enough to publish with a clear card

Keep w42 as research when the detectors and reports are informative but not yet
strong enough to move into another sibling project.

Retire or narrow w42 if rich strategy tags do not improve learning, do not produce
useful diagnostic reports, and do not clarify any book claims after fair tests.
Retirement should preserve detectors, negative results, and report artifacts so
the project does not relearn the same lesson later.

The first promote/retire pass kept w42 active as research. A follow-up promotion
moved its tracked code, reports, schemas, tables, and small checkpoints out of
`scratch/` into the top-level `w42/` directory. Deferred: HF artifacts, Gus
integration, Burl behavior changes, and any hard-coded book strategy rules.
