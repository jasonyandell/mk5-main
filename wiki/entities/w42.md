## Summary

w42 is the Winning 42 empirical strategy-validation workstream. It treats the
book as a source of hypotheses, not ground truth, and asks which human-legible
strategy concepts can become measurable detectors, report buckets, model
features, and paired oracle tests.

The workstream turns claims like "bid only enough," "donate count safely,"
"double ahead of your off," "create a pounce window," and "preserve the 84
stopper" into things the project can count, slice, falsify, and explain.

Read [[w42-book-validation]] for the curated path through the evidence pile.
This page is only the frontier hub.

## Boundary

[[gus]] remains the oracle-distillation sibling: a multi-head student trained
against [[forge]] E[Q] labels. w42 may borrow Gus corpus formats, public-state
encodings, and strategy-probe lessons, but it should not turn Gus into a mixed
research workbench.

[[burl]] remains the tool-using agent. w42 may inspect Burl traces when the
question is about reasoning or post-commit explanation, but W42 book claims do
not become Burl rules without an explicit later decision.

The current boundary decision is [[w42-promote-or-retire]]: keep w42 active as
research, promote durable code/reports into `w42/`, and do not promote research
models into Gus or Burl yet.

## Current Frontier

evidence and/or bounded blockers. [[w42-phase4-final-claim-audit]] is the closure
audit; [[w42-book-claim-synthesis-and-ai-directions]] is the current synthesis.

The live campaign is [[w42-book-validation-campaign]]. Wave 2 moved multiple

## Evaluation Principles

The first W42 data principle is to separate "can this claim be measured?" from
"does this feature improve a model?" Many book claims should become detectors,
tables, and reports before they become model inputs.

The second principle is that scalar E[Q] is not always enough. Branch-shaped
states need distribution evidence: make/set threshold mass, variance, quantiles,
lower-tail risk, branch labels, hidden-holder impact, and mitigation tags.

The third principle is public-state safety. Offline reports may use generated
sampled worlds to identify hidden-threat impact, but live features must come from
legal public state or learned beliefs.

The fourth principle is granularity. Single-decision probes are still the cheap
first pass, but plan-shaped claims need plan-shaped tests: state recognition,
plan state, future payoff, and comparison against a strong EV fallback.

## Report and Artifact Conventions

Expected locations:

- wiki narrative and decisions: `wiki/entities/w42.md` and
  `wiki/experiments/w42-*.md`
- durable code, local research scripts, and current artifacts: `w42/`
- temporary notebooks and unrelated one-off experiments: `scratch/`
- durable tables and figures: report paths named by the bead that creates the
  analysis
- W&B: training runs, sweeps, and metric panels once a model bead starts
- HuggingFace: durable datasets, model checkpoints, or public artifacts only
  after the data/model card is clear

Every report should state the question, data slice, detector or feature set,
baselines, metrics, claim-ledger impact, caveats, and exact commands or artifact
manifest.

## Claim Statuses

Use the conservative ledger vocabulary from [[winning42-strategy-measurement]]:

- `not-yet-tested` - detector or hypothesis exists, but no evidence run checked
  it
- `supported` - enumeration, oracle rollout, Gus/w42 probe, or Burl trace review
  supports the claim on the stated slice
- `contradicted` - evidence argues against the claim on the stated slice
- `context-limited` - the claim appears true only in a restricted regime
- `underpowered` - the sample or proxy is too weak for a verdict

Detector creation is not empirical support.
