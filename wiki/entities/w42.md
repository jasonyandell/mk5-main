## Summary

w42 is the Winning 42 empirical strategy-validation workstream. It treats the
book as a source of hypotheses, not ground truth, and asks which human-legible
strategy concepts can become measurable detectors, report buckets, model
features, and paired oracle tests.

The workstream turns claims like "bid only enough," "donate count safely,"
"double ahead of your off," "create a pounce window," and "preserve the 84
stopper" into things the project can count, slice, falsify, and explain.

Read [[w42-book-validation]] for the curated path through the evidence pile;
[[w42-book-claim-synthesis-and-ai-directions]] is the canonical synthesis of
what the work found. This page is only the frontier hub.

## Boundary

[[gus]] remains the oracle-distillation sibling: w42 may borrow Gus corpus
formats, public-state encodings, and strategy-probe lessons, but it does not
turn Gus into a mixed research workbench. [[burl]] remains the tool-using
agent: w42 may inspect Burl traces when the question is about reasoning or
explanation, but book claims do not become Burl rules without an explicit
later decision. The standing boundary decision is [[w42-promote-or-retire]]:
keep w42 as research, promote durable code/reports into `w42/`, and do not
promote research models into Gus or Burl yet.

w42 owns the charter, claim ledger, wiki pages, report conventions, detector
definitions, cheap supervised probes, and the workstream's
promotion/retirement decisions. It borrows [[forge]] E[Q] labels and
perfect-info oracle caveats, [[gus]] public-state corpora,
[[forge-analysis]] report discipline, and
[[winning42-strategy-measurement]]'s chapter inventory. It does not touch Gus
core training defaults, forge oracle semantics, Burl harness behavior, book
claims as hard-coded play rules, or private information unavailable from
legal public game state.

## Current Frontier

The phase-4 closure sweep established that all 64 book-claim ledger rows have
evidence and/or bounded blockers. [[w42-phase4-final-claim-audit]] is the
closure baseline; [[w42-book-claim-synthesis-and-ai-directions]] is the
canonical synthesis — the one page to read for what the book-validation work
found.

[[w42-book-validation-campaign]] is dormant: nothing has run since Wave 5
(2026-06-13) and research attention moved to the [[champion]]/[[jud]] line.
The campaign results that stand: bid-only-enough earned the first non-trivial
`supported` promotion; void creation split by seat position (follow survives,
lead fails); high-bid pounce was `contradicted` at the paired snapshot level;
[[w42-lens-v1-utility-head-to-head]] resolved the one-step utility contest
(EV beats robust_q25, CVaR, and p_make head-to-head). The campaign's deepest
lesson is the single-decision blind spot: most book claims are multi-step
plans probed at single-action granularity.

[[book-strategy-player]] was the promoted route (2026-05-04) for testing
those multi-step plans; it was never built — superseded before build by the
auction-first [[champion]]/[[jud]] redirection ([[champion-design-review]],
2026-06-09). [[w42-champion-teaching-battery]] later answered "does the
champion agree with the book?" directly against champion self-play
trajectories, bypassing BSP entirely.

The current frontier for "what is the best 42 player" is [[jud]] v1
([[w42-jud-v1]], graded 2026-07-06): the value-native bidder
`margin:wp`(head_8)+`lens:ev` is the best player the project knows how to
build, reconfirmed the same day by the Zeb-protocol measurement at `afd4802`
(eq n=10 still undefeated at pure play against every learned challenger since
Zeb; the `margin:wp` bidding crown holds against the live, non-distilled Gus
sim bidder too, +0.59 [-0.19,+1.39], 64 games). See [[champion]] and [[jud]]
for the full ladder.

The live w42 workstream is [[w42-book-second-pass]] (2026-07-07): a second
close reading of the book found the first extraction captured the tactics and
missed the information theory — the auction decoder, signaling conventions,
and match-score-conditioned bidding that map onto [[jud]]'s named untested
gaps.

Two detector-hygiene bugs remain important:

- `ch05_reckless_count` overfires when it cannot distinguish reckless count into
  bidder control from the broader regime.
- `ch05_setter_pressure_regime` is a regime feature, not an action endorsement.

## Evaluation Principles

- Separate "can this claim be measured?" from "does this feature improve a
  model?" — detectors, tables, and reports come before model inputs.
- Scalar E[Q] is not always enough: branch-shaped states need distribution
  evidence (threshold mass, quantiles, tail risk, hidden-holder impact).
- Public-state safety: offline reports may use sampled hidden worlds, but live
  features come only from legal public state or learned beliefs.
- Granularity: single-decision probes are the cheap first pass, but
  plan-shaped claims need plan-shaped tests.
