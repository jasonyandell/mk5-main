"Champion" named the unified belief-state player aspirationally, before the
artifact existed. The built thing is [[jud]] — home of the architecture, the
asset map, and the current-best-player fact. The rung-by-rung record of the
era that built the pieces (#20–#33) is [[champion-ladder]]. The goal the
player is graded against is [[the-wall]], which also carries the teaching half
as a declared side benefit.

The old name persists on two BUILT artifacts (names doctrine): the GitHub
milestone **Champion** — still the action ladder for this push — and the repo
directory `champion/` (utility, margin/jud nets, decoders). Wiki pages say
[[jud]].
